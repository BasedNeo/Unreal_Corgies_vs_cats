# B1 handoff — "The Vac-Tank" boss set piece (Wave 2)

**Status:** done for integration. The authoritative boss works end to end: it spawns as the skirmish finale, fights
with four telegraphed attacks, changes phase at 50 %, dies in a defeat sequence, and wins the match. The client model
(mech + pilot), the telegraph FX and a sim-driven lab are ready. **Lead wiring needed:** route `EntityKind.Boss` to
`createBossAvatar`, add the telegraph FX to `main.ts`, and hook up `?boss=1`. See §5.

**Design pitch (original IP).** Baron Von Floof is a smug, oversized Maine Coon. He wears a monocle on a chain, a
tilted crown, a lion mane and a crimson officer's jacket. He pilots a suburban robot vacuum that cats have turned into
a war machine:
- a tread undercarriage;
- a crimson disc body with a cat face on the bumper (glowing slit eyes, angry brows, whisker bars);
- spinning side brushes;
- taped-on cardboard armor with paw-print doodles and tally marks;
- a pen-style **laser-pointer cannon**;
- paper-towel-roll **hairball mortars**;
- a siren beacon;
- a domed dust-bin lid with cardboard cat ears, with his crowned head poking out of the hatch;
- a rear hatch full of **kittens**.

The running gag is that the cat is the one wielding the laser pointer, and he can't resist it: his face goes giddy
every time the dot runs. Nothing here references Conker/Rare bosses, names, jokes or presentation.

## 2. Design

**Body.** The boss is an `EntityKind.Boss` *character*: it has `char`, `health`, `combat.pve` and a ball collider
(r 2.5, centred 2.5 m above the feet). That one choice buys reuse of the combat lane:
- L3's hitscan, lag compensation, projectiles, blasts and bark blast all hit its capsule (a 5 m sphere);
- corgi bots perceive it and fight it with no AI change;
- `kill()` and the PvE-corpse path handle death and removal;
- `stepCharacter` moves it over the terrain with the KCC.

It picks its own heading, turn-rate limited (1.3 rad/s), and creeps forward while turning so clients see the body
rotate. Characters' KCCs ignore other characters, so the brain pushes them out of the hull every tick through their
own KCC. The push uses the pure `bossContactPush`, so no one walks through the tank and no one is pushed into walls.
The tank shrugs off knockback. It never regenerates (the combat lane's regen is kept off), and it avoids water and
jump pads via nav-cell cost.

**Movement AI.**
- Stays 10–19 m from its target and circle-strafes.
- Leashed to 42 m around its spawn so the fight stays in the arena.
- Checks its footprint against the nav grid: a disc of plain walkable cells, and the same sweep before moving.
- Has stuck recovery.
- Targeting: nearest, with aggro on whoever hurt it in the last 3 s, humans before bots (−8 m score), and a locked target while telegraphing.

**State machine** (flags bits 16–26 carry it to clients):

```
intro (drops 12 m, lands with `land`, invulnerable 2.4 s) → idle ─choose─▶ telegraph ─▶ active ─▶ recover (1.5 s punish window) ─▶ idle
  hp ≤ 50 %: phase shift (2.6 s: lid pops, taunt, 3 kittens at 1.4 s) → phase 2 (telegraphs/recover ÷1.3, move ×1.15, dmg ×1.1)
  dead: defeat (5 s: boom r 6.5, pilot ejects at 0.45 s, 3 secondary booms, taunt, removed)
```

| Attack (`ability` names) | Telegraph (p1 / p2) | Damage window | Avoid by | Damage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Laser Pointer Sweep**: `laser_pointer` → `laser_sweep` | 1.3 / 1.0 s. A red dot runs across the ground (8 / 10.4 m/s) and reaches the target ~80 % into the telegraph, then tracks it. **Fairness.**
- Nothing damages outside an `active` stage.
- Every telegraph is ≥ `MIN_TELEGRAPH` (0.8 s) in both phases; tests measure telegraph event → first hit.
- Hairball circles are truthful: the shells fly an exact analytic arc and land on the announced spot.
- Contact never deals damage.
- Leftover hairballs fizzle when the boss dies.

**Weak point.** The pilot takes ×2.
- For hitscan, the exact shot ray (from the `fire` event's eye to the recorded impact) is tested against the pilot capsule, using the boss position lag-compensated like L3's rewind.
- Phase 1, the head out of the hatch: `y ∈ [3.55, 4.85]`, r 0.62.
- Phase 2, lid off, the torso too: `y ∈ [3.0, 4.85]`, r 0.9.
- Projectile direct hits count when they strike the upper sphere cap.
- Blasts and abilities count as hull.
- L3's generic "top 20 % of the capsule" crit is undone and replaced, so `hit.crit` means *weak point* and the HUD's gold marker is right.
- The overlay in `artifacts/b1-lab-weak.png` shows the zones matching the model.

**HP.** `baseHp + hpPerExtraPlayer × (corgi combatants − 1)` = **8000 + 7300 per extra**. It counts players *and*
squad bots on the corgi team at spawn:
- 1 → 8k, 2 → 15.3k, 3 → 22.6k, 4 → 29.9k.
- The squad keeps a roughly constant size, because humans replace fill bots.
- Calibrated with headless bot fights (§6).

**Taunts** (`bark`, original lines, cooldown 7 s): intro, attack callouts, phase 2 ("My LID! Do you know what that lid
COST?"), kills ("Bad dog."), weak-point hurt, low hp, defeat ("Not the ejector seeeaaat!"), and victory.

## 3. Snapshot encoding (no protocol change)

| `EntityState` field | For `EntityKind.Boss` |
|---|---|
| `cls` | index into `BOSSES` (0 = Vac-Tank). Sim workaround: `e.cls = CLASS_IDS[idx]` so `Sim.toState` emits the index. |
| `x,y,z` | feet (ground contact), like characters |
| `yaw`, `pitch` | turret / laser aim (world). The chassis faces the velocity (entity-views' body-yaw logic already does that). |
| `flags` | EFlag bits as usual, plus `packBossFlags(attack 3 bits @16, stage 2 bits @19, phase2 @21, progress 5 bits @22)`. Flags are sent raw on the wire (checked `src/host/wire.ts`). |
| `hp`, `maxHp` | boss bar |
| `weapon`, `ammo` | −1 / 0 |

**Hairballs** are `EntityKind.Projectile` snapshot entities with `seed` = boss id and `weapon` = −1. L5's FX already
draws trails for them; `createBossTelegraphFx` draws the fuzzy balls. **Kittens** are ordinary cat bots.

## 4. APIs

```ts
// sim
bossSystems(): SimSystem[]                                   // 150 brain · 455 attacks · 560 damage (already in createDefaultSystems)
spawnBoss(sim, at?: {x, z, y?, yaw?}, opts?: { boss?: 'vac_tank'; players?: number; team?; drop?: boolean }): SimEntity
forceBossAttack(boss, 'laser' | 'mortar' | 'spin' | 'kittens'); skipBossIntro(sim, boss)      // tests / labs
livingBosses(sim): number; bossWaveStatus(sim, id): { alive, score }; countSquad(sim, bossTeam)
// shared
BOSSES, bossByIndex(cls), unpackBossFlags(flags), bossMuzzle(...), rayGround(...), bossContactPush(...), BOSS_ABILITY
// client
createBossAvatar({ boss: s.cls, seed: s.seed }): BossAvatar  // Avatar + { def, stats, mech, pilot, state, setExpression }
createBossTelegraphFx(scene, { heightAt }): { onGameEvent(ev), update(dt, states), stats, dispose() }
bossTelegraphFx(state, heightAt, t, out): TelegraphPrimitive[]  // pure: what to draw for one boss state
```

The avatar needs nothing beyond the usual `AvatarFrame`:
- `speed` drives treads and wheels;
- `aimYawOffset` / `aimPitch` drive turret and barrel;
- `hpFrac` drives the hit shudder, warm flash, a flapping plate below 35 % and the pilot's mood;
- `flags` carry the boss state;
- `dead` starts the defeat and eject.

`trigger()` accepts `'hit' | 'land' | 'death' | 'fire'` and the `BOSS_ABILITY` names; all of them are optional,
since state edges are inferred. `muzzleWorld()` returns the laser lens, so L5's laser tracers start there.
`height` = 5 m for the nameplate.

## 5. Wiring steps for the lead

1. **entity-views routing** (`src/client/views/entity-views.ts`):
   ```ts
   import { createBossAvatar } from '../procgen/boss';
   // in sync(): accept bosses and key them by boss index
   const isBoss = s.kind === EntityKind.Boss;
   if (!isBoss && s.kind !== EntityKind.Player && s.kind !== EntityKind.Bot) continue;
   const key = isBoss ? `boss:${s.cls}` : `${s.species}:${cls}:${s.team}:${s.seed}`;
   const avatar = isBoss ? createBossAvatar({ boss: s.cls, seed: s.seed }) : createAvatar({ ... });
   ```
   The existing body-yaw code is correct for bosses: they never set Aiming/Firing, so the chassis faces its velocity
   and the turret carries the aim through `aimYawOffset`. Optionally use a slower turn (λ 5 instead of 14) for bosses;
   the lab does.
2. **Telegraph FX + triggers** (`main.ts`):
   ```ts
   import { createBossTelegraphFx } from './procgen/boss';
   const bossFx = createBossTelegraphFx(ctx.scene, { heightAt: (x, z) => worldData.height(x, z) });
   bus.on('game', (ev) => { /* existing */ bossFx.onGameEvent(ev); if (ev.e === 'ability') views.trigger(ev.id, ev.ability); });
   // frame loop, after views.sync(states, …):
   bossFx.update(dt, states);
   ```
   Draw calls: 0 when idle, 1–4 while telegraphing. L5's FX needs nothing new:
   - laser `fire` events draw beams and impacts;
   - `explode` draws the booms;
   - `ability` rings and words, and `bark` plays cat gibberish.
3. **`?boss=1`**. Pick one:
   - (a) After the Room is created in `worker-host.ts` or `server/rooms.ts`, call `spawnBoss(room.sim)`. Once the sim has ticked, it picks an arena spot halfway between the cat spawns and the centre on clear lawn; for the West Yard that is near (25, 35).
   - (b) Make the boss the whole skirmish: `sim.state.matchConfig = { skirmish: { warmup: 3, waves: [{ counts: {}, boss: 'vac_tank', label: 'BOSS' }] } }`. The match then ends in victory on defeat.
4. **HUD (L5 or the lead)**:
   - a boss bar from the Boss state: `BOSSES[s.cls].name`, `hp/maxHp`, and phase 2 via `unpackBossFlags`;
   - `bark` subtitles: the lab shows them as "Baron Von Floof: “…”";
   - kill-feed name for boss kills: the boss is not in the roster;
   - optionally an objective override: while a boss lives the objective reads "FINAL WAVE (5/5) — 1 cat left".
5. **Prediction (L4)**: client prediction doesn't know the boss pushes, so a local player touching the hull is
   corrected by reconciliation. Fix: after `stepCharacter` in the predictor, apply `bossContactPush(def, boss…)` for
   interpolated boss states (a pure function in shared content).
6. **Docs**: add a B1 row to `PROGRESS.md`. `ARCHITECTURE.md` already lists `boss/`; add `src/client/procgen/boss/`.

## 8. Known issues / next

- **Needs human play**: balance is calibrated only against bots (§6). Watch squad-bot deaths: 7–19 per fight at
  4 bots. Bots respawn at the corgi base, about 100 m away.
- **One capsule per entity** (L3's model): the hit volume is a 5 m sphere. Shots through the air just above the deck
  near the pilot, or around the lower tread corners, register against the sphere. Hull vs weak point is exact.
  - A proper compound hit volume would need a combat-lane change: several capsules per entity, or a hit-test hook.
  - Proposal: `TargetSet` could accept per-entity hit shapes; the boss would register [disc, pilot].
- **Sim workaround**: `e.cls = CLASS_IDS[bossIndex]` so `Sim.toState` emits the BOSSES index. Proposal: let
  `toState` take `e.data.cls` for non-character kinds.
- **Laser vs elevated targets**: the dot runs on the terrain height, so players on props (picnic table, deck) are safe
  from the sweep. They are not safe from hairballs, which aim at feet height.
- **Client prediction** ignores the boss push-out (§5.5), so touching the hull shows a small correction.
- **Kittens** left alive when the boss dies stay in the world until the match ends or restarts; "the cats retreat" is
  only textual.
- **Vehicles (V1)**: kart vs boss contact is untested. The boss's KCC is blocked by Vehicle-layer colliders, and
  mounted riders are never pushed.
- The pilot's smug/furious expressions read best in close-ups; at gameplay range the face cue is mostly the crown,
  monocle and pose.

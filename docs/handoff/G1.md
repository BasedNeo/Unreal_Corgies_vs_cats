## Gameplay
**Concealment** (`concealmentAt` → `e.conceal.level`, order 620):
zone value (1 deep inside, fading over the outer 28 % of the ellipse; → 0 as the feet rise to grass height − 1 m,
so standing on a wall/pumpkin/ledge or jumping reveals you) × stance (**still 1 · sneaking** at walk speed **0.7,
0.85 with Crouch held · running 0.3 · sprinting 0**); **firing reveals for 2 s**, getting hit for 0.5 s.
- AI (`perceive`): a target of level *h* is spotted within `sight − (sight − 5 m)·h` (**5 m fully hidden**), ×2 when
  the bot was already tracking it; observers whose eye is > 3.5 m above the target's feet halve its concealment
  (the watchtower, teepee perch and bridge are real lookouts).
- Clients: level ≥ 0.5 sets **`EFlag.Stealthed`** in snapshots (no protocol change) → the existing nameplates hide
  hidden enemies and L5 footsteps drop to 35 %. The cloak keeps working (the flag is only cleared if we set it).

**Weather effects (authority + prediction, `stepWorldEffects`):**
- **Slippery grass:** grounded on terrain while wet (weather `wet`, or inside a running sprinkler's sweep) → the next
  tick moves with a cached immutable `MoveStats` variant: ground accel −60 %, decel −72 % at full wetness (8 levels).
  Measured: 6 ticks of running 2.8 vs 6.4 m/s, stopping slide 3.5× longer, same top speed. Props/decks keep grip.
- **Sprinkler jets:** inside the jet (9° cone or 0.65 m from its line) → shoved along it (55 m/s² × pressure),
  horizontal drag (0.88/tick), popped off the ground (3.6 m/s). Bursts: 24 s / 20 s every 140 / 170 s, never before
  75 / 130 s, 1.5 s pressure ramps, **rain sensor** (off while it rains).
- **Storm sight:** AI sight × `weather.sight` (clear 1 · overcast .96 · rain .85 · storm **.6**).
- **Environment clock:** the authority passes `sim.tick`; for human inputs the function uses the input's own
  `rt` (the tick the client was rendering) when it is within 30 ticks, else the tick. Client prediction calls it
  without a clock and uses `rt` → **prediction is bit-exact with no wiring** (tested through a rain transition and
  under sprinkler shoves). Bots (rt = 0) use the tick. `Room.stepExtra` falls back to the tick stamped on the entity.

**Weather state machine (`weather.ts`):** one 13-minute cycle per index k (seeded durations): clear 2–3.5 min (**cycle 0:
3–3.8 min, so every session starts clear and short tests/soaks see the L2 world**) → overcast 1–2 → rain 1.5–2.7 →
storm 1.2–2.2 (65 %; always in cycle 0) → clearing 1–1.5 → clear. Parameters (cloud, rain, storm, wind, wet, fog,
dark, sight) blend with smoothstep over ±10 s (≤ 0.075 per second). Lightning: per-second hashed strikes at
0.16 × storm/s, 150–1300 m away (thunder delay = dist/343 s), comic double-flicker flash. Time of day: 24-minute
day, 72 % daylight, starts 0.40–0.46 (late morning) so an 8-minute match ends in the golden hour.
**Determinism:** everything gameplay-relevant uses integer hashing and + − × ÷ floor sqrt only (Math.sin/cos/hypot are
not bit-identical across JS engines); the jet direction uses the polynomial `dsin/dcos`; a golden hash test pins it.

## Client APIs
- `createWorldView(scene, data, { …, grade?: pipeline.grade.uniforms, rainDrops? })`
- `worldView.update(dt, camera, tick?)` — **tick = server tick (float ok)**: weather, time of day and sprinkler jets
  follow `weatherAt/timeOfDayAt/sprinklerAt(seed, tick)`. Without a tick: L2 behaviour (clear, `daySpeed`).
- `worldView.setTimeOfDay(t)` pins the time (`?t=`); `pinTimeOfDay(false)` unpins. `setWeather('storm' | {...} | null)`
  overrides the look only (menus/labs). `worldView.weather` (WeatherSample), `worldView.tick`.
- `createWeatherAudio(audio.engine, worldData).update(worldView.weather, worldView.tick, camera, dt)` — uses only
  the public `AudioEngine` API (ctx, buses, `whenReady`, `play`); builds nothing before the gesture unlock.

## Known issues / next
- Concealment is binary on the wire (Stealthed ≥ 0.5); no client renders the local avatar translucent or shows a
  "hidden" HUD cue yet (lead/L1/L5). Tall grass does not part around characters (visual only).
- A character spawned *inside* tall grass is visible to the AI for its first tick (conceal runs at 620, after AI).
- Bots don't seek concealment or climb (2D nav: ledges/bridge/deck are obstacles); they do get shoved by sprinklers
  and slide in the rain (soaks stay deterministic). Tactical grass use for bots = L3 follow-up.
- Rain streaks are not occluded by roofs/decks; no ground splash sprites. Lightning is visual/audio only.
- Weather audio: unit-tested scheduling + a headless-Chromium check of the lab (`&audio&wx=storm`): AudioContext
  `running` after a click, storm loop levels rain .34 / roar .30 / wind .34, a `thunder` voice live ~1 s after strike
  #479 (352 m), no console errors. **A human still needs to listen** for mix balance (rain vs music, thunder loudness).
- Visual-only (walk-through) garden details: squash leaves, flowers, corn, tall grass, cage wires, teepee poles above
  3.2 m (their lower parts are colliders), roses, vines.
- `setWeather()` overrides visuals only; the sim always follows the clock. A client clock far from the authority
  (> 30 ticks of rt lag) can mispredict slip/jets during transitions/bursts (reconciled normally).

# L1 handoff — Characters & Animation

**Status:** done for Wave 1. `createAvatar()` now returns procedural, rigged, animated corgis and cats (6 cat
breeds, 3 corgi coats) in team armor with class headgear, backpacks and weapons. Everything is code-generated and
seeded, and it all goes through `toon()` / `glow()` / `addCreaseInk()`. The `Avatar` contract is unchanged.

**One blocker outside my lane (lead, 1 line):** `glow()` in `src/client/style/style-webgpu.js` renders **black**
on three r186. See [Integration notes](#integration-notes-for-the-lead) §1.

**Views in the character lab.** `view=grid|front|side|back|action|face|portrait|turntable|ots`. Other URL params:
- `anim=idle|walk|run|sprint|jump|fall|aim|aimfwd|fire|hit|death|emote|slide|swim|cycle`
- `species`, `cls`, `coat`, `team`, `expr`, `npc`
- `t=<s>`: pre-simulate that many seconds at 60 Hz, then freeze. This gives deterministic screenshots on a slow CPU.

**Animation layers** (in order). Every layer is shared by both species; only rest positions differ.
1. **Base locomotion:** idle blended with the walk/run cycle.
   - Idle: breathing, weight shift, seeded persona (head tilt, hip cock, stance width).
   - Walk/run: cadence scales with speed. Corgis waddle on stubby fast legs.
2. **Full-body overrides:**
   - **Corgi zoomies:** torso horizontal, rotary gallop on all fours, head up, ears pinned, tongue flapping, weapon slung.
   - **Cat sprint:** upright, leaning forward.
   - Air: tuck while rising, reach while falling.
   - Slide (belly sploot), swim (doggy paddle), drive (seated), emote (hop, left-paw wave, weapon cheer, butt wiggle).
3. **Upper-body aim:** pitch and twist spread over spine → chest → neck → head. The head always tracks the view. A bladed rifle stance is used while aiming.
4. **One-shots:** fire recoil, hit flinch (random side), double-jump forward flip, spring squash & stretch on jump and land.
5. **Death:** a "dead bug" flop onto the back with a bounce, legs twitching, X-eyes, tongue out, ears drooping.
6. **Spawn:** an elastic scale pop.
7. **Face:** mapped onto lid, brow, pupil, jaw, tongue and ear bones.
8. **Secondary springs:** ears (base + tip, driven by acceleration, vertical speed, turn rate and expression, plus idle twitches), tail chain (wag + lag), butt jiggle.
9. **Weapon placement in rig space.** The barrel follows exactly `aimPitch` / `aimYawOffset`. It blends between aim, low-ready, slung and cheer. Then two-bone IK puts both paws on the grips.

## Proof

- **Unit tests** — `npx vitest run tests/unit/characters` → `Test Files 1 passed (1) · Tests 26 passed (26)` (~4–7 s).
  - Each species × 4 classes × 2 seeds, on both tiers, checks:
    - triangles ≤ budget, measured from the real index counts;
    - draw calls ≤ 6 and bones ≤ 48;
    - CPU-skinned min.y within ±2 cm;
    - height 1.0–1.6 m;
    - snout z < −0.15 and muzzle z < −0.3.
  - Every class also builds within budget for both species on both tiers.
  - Coverage of the variant roster.
  - Materials come from the style system, `outlineReady` is set, and skin weights are normalized.
  - **Determinism**: a same-seed rebuild after the cache is freed gives an identical vertex hash, bone names and animated bone matrices.
  - Caching and refcounted `dispose()` behave correctly.
  - 300 frames of 14 states (including dt = 0 and dt = 0.5 spikes) produce no NaN, and walk/run stay grounded.
  - **The weapon points along the aim direction to within 0.05 rad.**
  - State-only hosts still get death and spawn reactions.
- **Audit** — `node tools/char-audit.mjs` → `all 24 character kits PASS (seeds 1,2,3)`.
  - Max triangles: hero **5,851 / 6,000** (corgi breacher), NPC **3,389 / 3,500** (corgi overwatch).
  - Draw calls ≤ 5, 45 bones, feet at −0.002…+0.002 m.
- **Typecheck** — `npx tsc --noEmit` is clean for the whole project. The lab is outside the tsconfig `include`; I typechecked it separately with a scratch tsconfig and it is also clean.
- **Performance** (Node, this shared 4-core box under heavy load from other lanes):
  - 24 animated avatars: **0.75 ms/frame (≈31 µs each)**.
  - Cold build ≈ 20–50 ms per new kit, shared afterwards. Use `prewarmCharacters()` at match load to avoid spawn hitches.
- **Draw calls per character:**
  - 1 skinned body (fur + face + gear share one vertex-color toon material);
  - 1 rigid weapon + 1 crease ink;
  - 1 weapon glow;
  - +1 visor glow for Overwatch.
  - Total ≤ 5. The outline pass redraws toon meshes, as it does for every asset.

**Honest self-critique.**

What works:
- The cast now reads as cute but tough. Big heads (≈37% of height) and big glossy eyes with highlights.
- Species are distinct at thumbnail size: huge upright corgi ears and fox muzzle versus round cat heads with whisker pads and tall pointed ears.
- Team colors pop, and every class is readable from behind (helmets, hats, packs, antenna, tank).
- The corgi zoomies drop onto all fours and read clearly as a dog gallop.
- The "dead bug" + X-eyes death is a clear comic read.
- Ink outlines are continuous on the skinned meshes. No torn hulls and no floating parts at rest.

Iteration history:
1. v1 (≈4/10): helmets covered the eyes, the weapon grip sat *inside* the vest (invisible arms), and the eyes were beady.
2. v2 fixed the seating of eyes, arms and headgear.
3. v3 replaced 100° lid shells, which made "eye cups" around every eye, with 75° caps that hide completely when open. It also fixed an inverted lid-slant sign, fixed a quaternion-aliasing bug that froze the weapon in low-ready while aiming, and made brows readable.

Remaining weaknesses:
- Toon bands show some faceting on cheeks and cranium in close-ups; this is the tessellation budget. It is invisible at the 4 m gameplay distance.
- Mouths are mostly hidden under the muzzle from the gameplay camera, so expressions rely on eyes, brows and ears. That reads well; the tongue is the main mouth signal.
- The idle is gentle rather than characterful.
- Glow parts render black until the style bug below is fixed.
- The owner's 1–10 scoring against the CHAR-01A target stills (MASTER_PLAN §6.8) has not happened yet. My own estimate is ~7/10 in the lab.

## Integration notes for the lead
1. **Style bug (blocks glow, not L1-owned):** `glow()` does `m.colorNode = vec3(new THREE.Color(color)).mul(intensity)`. In r186, `vec3(Color)` evaluates to **black**. Measured in the lab with the same mesh and pipeline:
   - `vec3(Color)` → rgb(0, 0, 0);
   - `uniform(new THREE.Color(c)).mul(k)` → rgb(255, 138, 97);
   - `vec3(1, .2, .2).mul(k)` → rgb(255, 200, 172).

   **Fix:** `m.colorNode = uniform(new THREE.Color(color)).mul(intensity)` (add `uniform` to the imports). Evidence: `artifacts/l1-glowbug-vec3color-black.png` vs `l1-glowbug-uniform-ok.png`. This affects every `glow()` in the game (lasers, tracers, cores).
2. **Triggers.** `main.ts` currently forwards only `jump` and `land`. For crisp reactions, also forward these `GameEvent`s:

   | Event | Call |
   |---|---|
   | `fire` | `views.trigger(ev.id, 'fire')` |
   | `hit` | `views.trigger(ev.dst, 'hit', ev.dmg / 20)` |
   | `death` | `views.trigger(ev.id, 'death')` |
   | `spawn` | `views.trigger(ev.id, 'spawn')` |
   | emote key | `'emote'` |

   Without triggers the avatar still infers these from `AvatarFrame` edges: dead flag → death, respawn → spawn, hpFrac drop → hit, grounded edges → jump/land, firing flag → auto recoil at ~8 Hz. A `jump` trigger while airborne plays the double-jump flip.
3. **`muzzleWorld()`** is accurate: it updates the parent chain, so use it for tracers and muzzle flashes. **`height`** is the head top (1.15–1.25 m) for nameplates.
4. **`prewarmCharacters(kits)`** should run during match load with the known roster, to avoid a ~20–50 ms first-spawn hitch per kit.
5. **Tiers.** `isLocal` selects the hero tier (≤ 6k); bots and remote players get the NPC tier (≤ 3.5k).
6. **Culling.** Characters use a static bounding sphere (r = 1.7 m) because poses leave the bind box.
7. **Bone TRS.** Bones use `matrixAutoUpdate = false`. Their `.matrix` is authoritative; `.position` / `.quaternion` are not maintained.
8. **Docs to update.** `ARCHITECTURE.md` should list `src/client/anim/` (rig, animator, face, springs) and the `procgen/characters/*` files above. `PROGRESS.md` should get the L1 row. Neither is in my paths, so I did not edit them. MASTER_PLAN's L1 card says `char.html`; the lab lives at `labs/characters.html`.
9. **Dependencies.** No new npm dependencies. `tools/char-audit.mjs` uses the already-installed `tsx` API.

## Known issues / next ideas
- Glow parts are black until the style fix in item 1 above.
- Crowd LOD by distance (MASTER_PLAN §6.7) is not implemented; only hero/NPC tiers by `isLocal`. Next: switch tier at ~25 m, or bake instanced far crowds.
- No foot IK on slopes. Feet plant at root height, so they may float or sink by a few cm on steep terrain.
- The hit reaction side is random. The `hit` event's source direction could be passed in via a future `trigger` strength/direction extension, which would be a lead contract change.
- The boss Maine Coon (§6.1, ≤ 15k) is not built. The plan tables in `species.ts` are ready for a `mainecoon` plan.
- There is no automated 64 px silhouette-distinctness check yet (§6.6). The lab `grid` view is the manual check.
- Idle/emote variety could grow: sniff, scratch, stretch, a corgi "sploot" idle.

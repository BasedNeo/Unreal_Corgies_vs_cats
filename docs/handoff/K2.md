# K2 handoff: HARDENED characters (Wave 7, Block 1)

**Status:** done, not committed (the lead integrates). Corgis and cats are now armoured veterans: plate carriers in
faction camo over a dark under-suit, shoulder plates, bracers, knee pads, boots, a utility belt, webbing, dog tags,
scars, a notched ear, wet and muddy fur, a steady squint under heavy brows. They keep their animal soul (short corgi
legs, big ears, fox muzzle, fluffy rump and tail; lithe cats with tails, ears and whisker pads) and K1's comedy (kill
grin, derp, terrified, the dead-bug knockout with X eyes). The team signal moved off the camo onto shoulder shells,
armbands, helmet bands, class markings and glowing team lamps. An elite variant per side exists (`veteran: true`).
The bosses got a cheap matching pass. C3's looks still apply.

The `Avatar` contract is unchanged; two lead snippets below (veteran wiring, docs).

## 6. For the lead

1. **Veteran wiring (contract, lead-owned).** `src/client/views/avatar.ts`:
   ```ts

   ```
   `src/client/views/entity-views.ts` (rebuild on change: it is a different kit):
   ```ts
   import { isVeteranSeed } from '../procgen/characters';
   const veteran = !isBoss && (this.opts.veteranOf?.(id) ?? (s.kind === EntityKind.Bot && isVeteranSeed(s.seed)));
   const key = isBoss ? `boss:${s.cls}:${s.seed}` : `${s.species}:${cls}:${s.team}:${s.seed}${veteran ? ':vet' : ''}`;

   ```
   For players, a roster flag or a C3-style earned look would do; proposal for `src/shared/content/cosmetics.ts`
   (C3's file): a `rank` slot with `rank_none` (default) and `rank_veteran` (unlock `{ kind: 'level', level: 10 }`),
   resolved by `sanitizeLook` like the others, and `veteran: look.rank === 'rank_veteran'` at view creation.
   Pass `veteran` to `prewarmCharacters` for the roster too (each veteran kit is a 20–50 ms cold build).
2. **Style (S4).** The body uses `toon({ vertexColors, surfaceAttr: true, surface: 'fur', mud: 0.3 })` and every body,
   neckwear and boss-fur geometry now carries the `surface` vec4 attribute (`MeshBuilder.surface`). If S4 renames
   `surfaceAttr` or the `SURFACES` presets, only `characters/index.ts` and `SURF` in `mesh-builder.ts` change. Any mesh
   drawn with the body material must carry the attribute (the weapon now has its own material for that reason). Token
   names read: `hazardOchre, camoBlack, gunmetal, steel, oxblood, charcoal, brass, olive, khaki, underSuit, mud`.
3. **Weapons (X3).** `characters/index.ts` gives the weapon `toon({ surface: 'weapon', ...kit.weapon.finish })`. X3 could

   The kit budget includes the weapon: NPC margin is 20 triangles on the worst kit.
4. **Team lamps** are one extra draw per non-Overwatch character (see §3). The Overwatch beacon and monocle are now team
   coloured (blue for corgis) instead of laser red.
5. **Docs** (not my paths): `ARCHITECTURE.md` (BodyDress, gear.ts faction/team split, `MeshBuilder.surface`, `veteran`),
   `PROGRESS.md` (the K2 row), `docs/handoff/L1.md` (bones 47, kit cache includes `:vet`).

## 7. Known issues / next
- **Dusk front light.** Seen from the front (away from S4's key light) the dark suit and armour go dim; the ochre /
  oxblood plates, the team shells and the lamps carry the read. If playtests want more, a slightly lighter under-suit
  (`underSuit` token) is the cheapest lever.
- **Scars are kit-level**, not per seed (geometry is shared per kit): the scar side varies by class, not by bot.
- **No reload animation exists** in the animator, so "no tearing in reload" is untested; fire, emote, sprint, zoomies,
  jump and the knockout are.
- **Warden** is now the most team-coloured class (its cone dish, 23–43 % of visible cells): intentional (high-vis riot
  gear), but the cone flare is also 6 % wider, so K1's "cone near the crosshair" note deserves a playtest glance.
- **Corgi cheeks** are still white puffs from the front on red and sable coats (real corgis), the one cute cue left.
- NPC-tier budget is nearly full again (§3).

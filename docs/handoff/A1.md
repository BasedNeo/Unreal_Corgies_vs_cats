# A1 handoff: adventure framework + chapters 1–2 ("The Last Tennis Ball")

**Status: done, ready to integrate.** Mode `adventure` runs chapter data (reach / interact / hold / collect / defeat /
destroy / survive), step spawns, a stealth alarm, checkpoints with fail forward, and briefing → live → complete (time
vs par → paw medal). It folds into `MatchState` and drives an S1-style beacon, so the existing HUD line, beacon, E
prompt and mission card follow chapter steps. Bots follow every step type. Client: intro/outro comic captions, step
barks, a squad-down beat, the chapter-complete card, sentry cones, catnip bags, device progress, and the ADVENTURE
chapter picker in the menu.

**No shared contract changed.** All lead-owned edits are paste-ready in §3. Every one was applied to a scratch copy
(`git archive HEAD` + my files + §3) where tsc is clean, the tests pass and the screenshots were taken.

- **Spotted = the alarm, not a fail.** A cat turning `EFlag.Alerted` (sight, hearing gunfire, getting shot) during a
  stealth step:
  - spawns that step's `alarm` hunters, once;
  - turns every sentry into a hunter;
  - makes the cat yell a line;
  - turns the beacon red and appends "· ALARM!" to the objective line.
- The start is > 50 m from every sentry, so the pups hanging back there never see one (sight range 45 m).

### Shared rules
- **Briefing.** The briefing runs for 7 s: MatchState `warmup`, no combat.
  - The squad gathers at the chapter start and the intro panel plays.
  - Every human pressing E skips it.
  - The countdown waits for a human to join (a slow page load), for 10 s at most.
- **Step feedback.**
  - Each step gives +10 team points (`score`, reason `step`) and 50 roster points to whoever did it.
  - Step barks are spoken by squad members in turn (`bark` events). The avatars act them out, and the client shows
    them as speech bubbles.
- **Fail forward.**
  - A squad wipe (every corgi down at once) starts a 3 s beat ("SQUAD DOWN! Back to the checkpoint in 3…").
    Respawns are held during the beat.
  - After the beat, the squad restarts at the last checkpoint step: step 0, or a step with `checkpoint`.
  - What comes back: that checkpoint's cats, at the exact positions they had when it began (sentries with their
    loops), its counters and items, and the squad at its rally point.
  - There are unlimited retries. The chapter never restarts from zero.
- **Respawns.** Individual respawns and late joiners appear at the squad's rally point: the last target reached, or
  where the last bag was taken.
- **Complete.** The chapter-complete result:
  - The time runs from live, including the fail beats.
  - The medal: gold ≤ par, silver ≤ 1.5 × par, bronze otherwise.
  - Rewards: +50 (`score`, reason `chapter`); MatchState `ended` with winner Corgis; the remaining cats are removed.
  - After 30 s the room loads the next playable chapter, or replays the last one. This is for online co-op; offline,
    the card's NEXT CHAPTER reloads into it straight away.
- **Bots help, never steal the win.** With a human in the squad:
  - Only a human completes reach and interact steps. The pups escort, and wait in the zone.
  - In a stealth step, before the alarm, the pups hang back at the rally point so the human leads. They join once
    the alarm is up.
  - Without a human, the pups do everything: reach, the runner uses interact points (including the kiosk), one bag
    each, hunt the targets, hold and survive zones.
  - The `vehicle` and `airborne` reach rules are waived only for a bot-only squad.
- **Tutorial pacing.** The chapter-1 cats never use abilities. Later chapters keep C2's archetype rolls.

## 4. Guide for A2 (chapters 3–6)
### 4.1 Writing a chapter
Append a `ChapterDef` to `CHAPTERS` in `src/shared/content/chapters.ts`. Match its `CHAPTER_PLAN` slot exactly: id,
title, cls, district. The picker, the card's NEXT CHAPTER button and the online room advance pick it up by
themselves. `tests/unit/adventure-chapters.test.ts › chapter data` checks every chapter in `CHAPTERS`:
- reach/interact/hold points have walkable ground inside the radius;
- collect spots are on walkable main-region cells, clear of colliders and > 5 m apart;
- spawn archetypes are known and placeable;
- stealth steps have an alarm;
- captions are 2–3 lines of ≤ 80 characters.

Example: chapter 3.

### 4.3 Rules of thumb (from chapters 1–2)
- **Measure LOS and range from the rally point to every sentry.** Pups at the start see 45 m in clear weather. One
  that sees a sentry shoots, the noise wakes the garden, and the human's stealth is gone. Chapter 2's start sits
  56 m from the nearest sentry for this reason.
- **Human arrival is the success.** Put the first reach within ~25 m of the start (FTUE: first success in 60 s).
- **Par ≈ 2× the bot-only time.** Pups skip reading, sneaking and kit picking. Chapter 1: bots 52 s → par 120 s.
  Chapter 2: bots 80 s → par 170 s. A2's bar is "bots inside 2× par".
- Split a big fight into two spawn groups at different distances, so it arrives in halves.
- Use `checkpoint` on the step before anything that can wipe the squad.
- Test a chapter headless with the pattern in `adventure-chapters.test.ts`:
  `new Room(sim, { mode: 'adventure', chapter, botsPerTeam: [4, 0] })`, then tick until `adventureState(sim).phase ===
  'complete'`.

## 6. Known issues / follow-ups
- **Sentries jog their loop.** The brain has no walk intent for goals, so they pace at run speed. That looks busy,
  and running also affects nothing else. Proposal: a `t.walk` hint that the brain honours in patrol (brain.ts is
  C2's/lead's).
- **Bots can't shoot destructibles.** The brain targets characters only, so a bot-only `destroy` step relies on
  breacher charge fuses (≤ 30 s each) or other blasts. A2's "chapter 3 completable by bots inside 2× par" will need
  a brain hook like `droneShot` for props (an X1/C2 item).
- **Bots don't drive or glide on purpose.** `vehicle`/`airborne` reach steps are waived only for a bot-only squad. An
  AFK human blocks those steps, and interact and reach steps too, by design.
- **One adventure chapter per page on the client.** `setActiveAdventureChain` is module state in
  `shared/content/objectives.ts`. Only the client reads it; the authority keeps its own runtime per Sim. A stateless
  alternative: pass `beacon.seed` into `objectiveChainByIndex(cls, seed)` in S1's three client call sites.
- **Stealth escalates.** If cats are still alerted when the next stealth step starts, its alarm fires at once: a loud
  squad keeps the garden awake. Chapter 2 bot-only runs raise 3 alarms and are still completed.
- **Picker locks are cosmetic.** A `?chapter=` link plays any chapter (the authority never gates on progress).
- **Captions pace by frame time.** They follow frame `dt` (capped at 0.25 s per frame), so a page that stalls
  doesn't skip lines. On a 1–2 fps SwiftShader page they read slower than real time.
- **Cones draw over the world.** Sentry cones render with the depth test off (owned copies of the style's glow
  material), so they show through tall grass, and also through walls. That is intended readability during stealth
  steps only.
- **Spawn facing races in every mode** (see §3.2): adventure uses `onChapter`; the general fix is the lead's.
- **Chapter-cat kills score for the team.** Each chapter cat down is +1 team point (`score` reason `kill`), as in the
  skirmish, so the audio plays its score sting per kill.

**Status: done for integration. Nothing is staged or committed.** The lead integrates this work.
- Every number below comes from a clean snapshot of HEAD `b128cb5` plus only my files (`git archive HEAD`, my files

What a player gets: a seventh chapter, the adventure's first on The Lot. It unlocks after chapter 6 (the picker's
existing rule), plays at the Lot's dusk with the floodlights on, and runs about 2–4 minutes:
1. **The Pipeworks sneak.** Squeeze in under the site gate, cross the causeway, go through a dark pipe, reach the foot
   of the spoil heap. Three sentries pace the open ground. Being seen only brings the heap's night shift hunting.
2. **The siren fuse.** Two kittens stand in the floodlight pools on the heap. Get round the back of the east tower and
   pull the siren fuse from its ballast box (E).
3. **The stash.** Four tennis balls around the scaffold. The night shift turns up anyway.
4. **Hold the scaffold** for 40 s against the counter-attack: a shield-carrying Heavy, tabbies, kittens and a Siamese
   lookout. The Lot's rain usually rolls in around here.
5. **The getaway.** Take the cats' site kart from the heap's west side and drive the stash home to the corgis' pit
   (~230 m, chasers on the way).

The featured kit is the **Infiltrator**. Its 5 s cloak is how a sneak goes clean: once over the causeway, once (after a
wait in the dark pipe for the cooldown) across the mud, once round the tower. Any kit can finish the chapter.

## 2. Changes from the card's beats, and why

| "Steal the stash from the heap top, **up the scaffold**" | the stash on the heap top round the scaffold (one ball under the deck) | Collect spots carry no height (`{ x, z }`) and items sit ≤ 2 m over the ground. The scaffold is where the hold happens. |

## 3. How it works

### Data
`chapters-lot.ts` imports only types from `chapters.ts` (which imports it): no runtime cycle. No new contract field:
`map` is C10's, and everything else is chapters 1–6's contract.

### The runner is map-generic (`src/sim/adventure/runner.ts`)
A sim builds its world once, for the room's map. So the runner only ever loads a chapter of its sim's own map:
- `chapterFitsSim(sim, def)`: the sim's `worldData.map` is `sanitizeMap(def.map)`. A hand-made test world (no registry
  id) plays anything, as before.
- `chapterForSim(sim, def)`: the setup system loads the room's chapter if it fits, else this map's first chapter. A
  West Yard room asked for chapter 7 plays chapter 1; a Lot room asked for a West Yard chapter plays chapter 7. The
  soak's chapter-less adventure room on The Lot therefore plays chapter 7. `adventureConfig.chapter` (tests) is used as
  given.
- `chapterAfterOnMap(sim, def)`: an online room moves on in `roomChapterAfter`'s order, skipping other maps' chapters.
  West Yard: 1 → 2 → … → 6 → 1, as before. The Lot: 7 → 7.

Nothing else in the runner assumed the West Yard: formation spots, rally points, spawns, sentry loops, parked vehicles
and items all read the sim's own world and nav grid. The six West Yard chapters load and advance exactly as before
(§4.4).

How the stealth layout was found (scratch runs, 8 seeds each, `scratchpad/a7/stealth-v*.log`):
- v4, sentries by the causeway and in the mud: a stand-in that read the cones was still seen 8/8, on the approach and
  **through the pipes' gap windows**. A pipe's 1.8 m gaps and its mouths leak sight at < ~17° off their axis.
- A sentry walking its loop turns ~2.4 rad in 0.25 s. At 45 m sight in the open, nobody (stand-in or player) can
  sneak past a sentry within range by watching its cone. So the approach must stay out of range, the pipes must be
  unseeable, and the one open crossing must fit the featured kit's cloak.
- v6 (shipped): approach ≥ 53.7 m from every post; interior 8/8 hidden; blind sprint seen 7/8; two-cloak plan unseen
  7/8; the long way round the east flank seen 8/8. The siren with kittens in the pools: straight at it seen 6/8, a

   the Siamese or one grunt.

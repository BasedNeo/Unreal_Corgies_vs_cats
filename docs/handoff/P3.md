# P3 handoff — the render budget pass (Wave 7, W7 P3)

**Status: done; nothing is staged or committed.** The lead integrates this work. Every view in the card is inside its
target. The live 4v4 TDM went from 328 draws / 1.52 M triangles to **262 / 1.19 M**. The 12v12 went from 514 / 1.69 M
to **373 / 1.32 M**. The Lot at 14v14 went from 449 draws to **331**. The low tier costs less than before.

Three changes did it:
- an **ink LOD** in the scene pass, which skips an outline hull that would be under 0.3 px wide;
- a **character detail LOD** past 20 m;
- **distance-culled crease-ink tiles** for world props.

Nothing changes within 18.5 m of the camera. Characters keep their silhouette ink at every distance, because the class
read depends on it (§2, loop 1b). No sim, host, shared or protocol file was touched.

## 2. The changes, one loop at a time, each measured

Each loop is one change, measured with `perf-render.mjs` on scratch copies of the clean tree plus that loop's files (same
queries). The deltas include the ±5-draw noise of moving bots.

| Loop | 4v4 draws / tris | 12v12 draws / tris | The Lot 14v14 draws / tris |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before | 328 / 1,522 k | 514 / 1,692 k | 449 / 841 k |
| 1. Ink LOD (+ `keepInk` on characters) | 283 / 1,363 k (−45 / −159 k) | 450 / 1,531 k (−64 / −160 k) | 411 / 796 k (−38 / −45 k) |
| 2. Character detail LOD | 262 / 1,355 k (−21 / −8 k) | 383 / 1,500 k (−67 / −31 k) | 337 / 753 k (−74 / −43 k) |
| 3. Crease-ink tiles + destructibles' crease distance (= after) | 262 / 1,187 k (0 / −168 k) | 373 / 1,322 k (−10 / −178 k) | 331 / 695 k (−6 / −58 k) |

### Loop 1: the ink LOD (`src/client/engine/renderer.ts`)

The toon outline pass (three r186 `ToonOutlinePassNode`) draws every toon mesh twice: the ink hull, then the mesh. Past the
style's far cap, the hull thins as 1/d. At a 720 px buffer it is under 0.3 px wide beyond 18.7 m: a broken speckle that
covers under a third of the silhouette pixels.

`installInkLod()` replaces the pass's per-object render function with the same function (same hull material, thickness,
far cap and hull-then-mesh order), with two additions:
- **Sub-pixel hulls are skipped.** Width = thickness × min(d, farCap) / d × bufferHeight / 2, and d is measured to the
  object's world bounding sphere. The hull is skipped when it would be under `INK_MIN_PX` (0.3) wide.
- **`userData.drawDistance` (m)** skips an object past that distance. Loop 3 uses it.

`ctx.inkLod` reports the cut distance and the drawn and skipped counts for each frame. `createRenderContext({ inkMinPx })`
sets the threshold, and `&inkmin=0` in the characters, look and world labs turns the LOD off for an A/B.

Two groups always keep their hull:
- objects with `frustumCulled = false`: debris, kart wheels and pooled FX, whose bounds can be stale;
- meshes flagged `userData.keepInk` (loop 1b).

What it cut: whole 100 m prop cells whose nearest point is beyond the cut, far fences and kiosks, vehicles, and neckwear
at range. Pure helpers (`inkWidthPx`, `inkCutDistance`, `distanceToBounds`) are unit-tested.

### Loop 1b: a regression the screenshots caught, and `keepInk`

The first version also cut character hulls. At 35 m the Overwatch's whip mast **vanished**, and at 22 m it was broken
(`artifacts/p3/compare/mast-35m-no-keepink.png`). A mast about 0.4 px wide is a thin vertical line, so it rasterizes all
or nothing per pixel column. Its 0.16 px hull roughly doubles the chance that it covers a column, so at range the hull
*is* the silhouette. K2 relies on masts, charges and barrels to carry the class read at 35 m.

The character body and weapon now set `userData.keepInk`, so their ink is exactly as before at every distance. The
neckwear hull still follows the rule. After the fix, the 35 m lineup differs from before only by a few weapon crease
pixels (loop 2), and the mean pixel difference (3.28) is at the film-grain floor
(`compare/mast-35m-final.png`, `compare/lineup-35m-final.png`).

### Loop 2: the character detail LOD (`src/client/procgen/characters/index.ts`)

Past `CHARACTER_DETAIL_DISTANCE` (20 m, with a ±1.5 m band so a pet at the threshold never flickers), a character:
- hides its weapon crease ink (≈ 1.2 k triangles, the lines on a gun about 25 px long);
- hides its weapon glow (a sight or emitter, a pixel or two);
- stops casting weapon and neckwear shadows.

The body, weapon, team lamps, neckwear, body shadow and the body and weapon ink all stay, so team, class and look read
the same. The level is chosen by the camera that draws the body: its `onBeforeRender` runs only for perspective cameras
and never for the sun's orthographic shadow camera. It is exposed as `CharacterAvatar.detail`, which labs and tests can
force.

The object tree never changes, so `stats.drawCalls`, `char-audit` and the C3 and K2 draw checks are as before.

Per NPC character with a neckwear (unit-tested, every class × species):
- near: 6 scene + 3 hull + 3 shadow draws, 9.7–11.1 k triangles;
- far: 4 + 2 + 1 draws, 8.4–9.7 k triangles.

`entity-views.ts` needed no edit.

### Loop 3: crease-ink tiles (`src/client/world/prim-mesh.ts`, `destruct-view.ts`)

A merged cell's crease lines (`solid` group) are split into tiles a third of the cell wide (33 m). Each tile is a
`LineSegments2` child with `styleInk` (so the tier's live prop-crease toggle still works) and
`drawDistance = CREASE_DRAW_DISTANCE` (40 m to the tile's bounds). From a West Yard spawn, 35 % of the 27.7 k prop
crease segments are within range (unit test), at about the same number of crease draws as before.

The destructibles' single crease set also gets the 40 m draw distance. Its bounds are where the stacks stand, around the
Garage, so it no longer draws 44 k triangles of tuna-can creases (including parked rubble) from the other end of the
yard. X1's contract is kept: at most 3 meshes, 1 line set and 4 draws. Far scenery beyond the yard fence never had creases.

## 6. Left over, not taken, and decisions

**Nothing is over budget at the card's views.** The tightest margin is 12v12 draws: 373 of 400.

Cheap levers that were measured but not taken (the targets did not need them), largest first:
- **Shadow pass** (345–377 k triangles): now the biggest pass. Prop cells are 100 m, so the 124 m shadow box always
  takes whole cells, and the `noink` detail groups (decals, labels, cables, tuna, stencils) cast shadows too: about
  47 k triangles and 6 draws in the 4v4 frame. Turning off `noink` casting is one line in `prim-mesh.ts` and
  `destruct-view.ts`, but thin masts and cables would lose their shadow lines.
- **Foliage** fills a 58 m circle around the camera, and about two thirds of it is behind the camera. That is ~65 k
  triangles, and a view-cone refill would save ~40 k.
- **Fence boards**: one instanced mesh around the yard, 26 k triangles × 3 passes. Splitting it per side would let the
  frustum and shadow culls work.
- **Terrain**: nine 1 m chunks (115 k) at any distance. A 2 m LOD for far chunks would save 60–80 k, but visual ==
  collider must stay exact near players.
- **A far character body** (a third kit tier of ~1.5 k triangles) would save ~3 k × 3 passes per far pet. It needs K1
  silhouette checks at range.

Decisions for the owner and the lead:
1. **Owner, the look at range.** Props 40–100 m away lose their 1.1 px crease hatching (visible on the swing set, the
   garden trellis and the FOB house's window frames), and prop hulls past 19–28 m (depending on the screen) lose their
   sub-pixel speckle. Characters are unchanged. The knobs are `CREASE_DRAW_DISTANCE` (40) and `INK_MIN_PX` (0.3). On a real GPU, compare with `&inkmin=0`
   in `labs/look.html` or `labs/world.html`.
2. **Lead, docs** (not my paths): the W7 gallery budget table, a PROGRESS row and ARCHITECTURE lines are paste-ready below.
3. **Lead, optional in-game A/B** in `main.ts`, for the real-GPU check:
   `createRenderContext(app, { forceWebGL: …, quality: …, inkMinPx: params.has('inkmin') ? Number(params.get('inkmin')) : undefined })`.
4. **No real-GPU timings.** SwiftShader measures draws and triangles only. The fewer hulls and crease tiles should also
   cut vertex work, but that is unmeasured.

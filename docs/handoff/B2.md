What a ride looks like: a room bot whose trip is ≥ 45 m away checks every 0.5 s whether walking to a kart (or its
Kart-O-Matic: walk up, E vends, E boards) plus driving beats walking by ≥ 0.5 s. If it does, the bot boards and drives.

5 m/s near the trip's end. An enemy seen within reach is rammed when the estimate says it pays: a straight run-up, the

enemy within 14 m that can't be rammed means hop out and fight.

## 5. Known issues

- **Ram kills are rare.** Rams land in every match; kills are occasional: 3 in 4 × 180 s matches, and 1 in every 3
  to 6 short (45 s) ones.
  - The brain's engaged bots back straight off from a kart's rider at run speed (they keep their preferred range).
    Even dead-centre rams then close at ~12 m/s and deal 51–63 on 140–160 hp targets.
  - Ram picks already prefer hurt targets (finishing blows), pets facing away and targets not moving away.
  - A real fix is in the brain's reaction to karts: sidestep instead of backing off, or a kart-aware threat range.
    That's not in B2's paths.
- **The plane's gun rarely kills.** 0–3 hits per sortie in TDM; bails and crashes are fine (0 crashes in all runs).
  Targets on foot move, and the gun has a tight gimbal. Strafing only picks enemies on foot in the open (not karts,
  not planes).
- **Cross-sim determinism leak (pre-existing, not B2; fixed by D1, `docs/handoff/D1.md`).** On the committed tree without the hook, running a 45 s TDM
  Room before a bot-only chapter 6 in the same process changes ch6's step times from step 2 on (after the flying).
  Two identical back-to-back runs agree. The determinism tests here (and N1's) compare repeats of one scenario, so
  they don't see it. Some per-world cache holds per-sim state; I did not find which.
- **One-off costs.** Kart grid 60–100 ms per world on first need (plus the nav grid's 180–200 ms). Rebuild ~12 ms each
  time a destructible changes the walkable count.
- **Tick cost with the hook.** 28 characters: tick p95 2.33 ms vs 1.57 with vehicles off (limit 3 ms; load 9–10 on
  4 cores). The +0.76 ms splits between the AI (+0.33 ms p95: driving, ram and dive checks) and the kart/plane step
  systems, which now have vehicles to step (p95 0.49 ms). The 4v4 soaks stay at p95 ≤ 1.40 ms.
- **Pups** (adventure) only drive in a `vehicle` step and never with a human in the squad; they don't take karts to
  travel. Wave cats never drive.
- **Karts only on the ground grid.** Decks and links (N1) are for pets; karts don't climb. A trip whose goal is up on
  a deck walks.
- **No kart for a human teammate** is a heuristic: within 5 m of it, walking at it from 25 m, or looking at it from
  14 m. A human who wants a kart from farther away may see a bot take it.

## 6. How B2b reuses B2a (and what else can)

- **One state machine.** `vehicleThink` runs both. `RideState` phases (idle → board → drive, plus leap for the glide)
  and the boarding code (walk up, E to vend, release, E to board, claims, `humanWants`, `endRide`, cooldowns) are
  shared. The seat decides: `driveTick` for a kart, `planeTick` for the plane.
- **Planes fly on R1's `planeAutopilot`.** `flyPlane` (in `drive.ts`) turns a `FlightGoal` (flyover, land, strafe)
  into an autopilot goal each tick. Strafing overrides it only while a dive is on, with the same `InputCmd`
  (throttle, yaw/pitch command, Fire) a human flies with.
- **Anything that needs a bot at a place fast** can set a tactics trip ≥ 45 m away; the kart choice is automatic. For
  a scripted drive, call `driveKart(sim, kart, driver, {x, z, r, stop}, grid, budget, controls)` and
  `controlsToInput`. It depends only on sim state and the `DriverState` you pass (deterministic).

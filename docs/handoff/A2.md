# A2 handoff: chapters 3–6 of "The Last Tennis Ball"

**Status: done, ready to integrate.** Chapters 3–6 are data in `src/shared/content/chapters.ts`, run by A1's runner
with a few optional additions. Every chapter is completed by a bot-only squad inside its par (not just 2× par), on
6/6 seeds, and replays identically. Each chapter's featured kit or vehicle is what gets a step done, proven with
scripted human stand-ins on the real West Yard.

Bot-side gaps closed:
- bots shoot props in a `destroy` step: a minimal brain hook next to C2's drone shot;
- Breachers plant Dig Charges where X1's breach fuse actually fires;
- sentries walk their posts;
- the squad's pups carry the chapter's kit.

**No shared contract changed**, and I touched no file outside my paths. There is one small paste-ready lead edit
(§4, the roster kit), plus doc lines for the lead (§7).

## 1. What players get

Shared rules, added to A1's (all optional fields; chapters 1–2 are unchanged):
- **Human-only rules, and the grace.** `vehicle`, `airborne` and the new `minY` (feet that high, e.g. a roof) hold for
  humans. A squad with no human has them waived (A1's rule, now also for `minY`).
- **Fail forward.** After **120 s** on one step, those rules relax for humans too, and a pup says *"Forget style
  points. Just get there, we will say you flew."* So a player without the kit, or without the skill yet, is never
  stuck.
- **Pup kits.** Pups carry the chapter's kits (`pups`), featured kit first. Humans replace pups from the end, so the
  featured-kit pups stay. Humans keep their own kit (the picker already gives them the featured one).

## 2. How it works

### Contract additions (`chapters.ts`, all optional, backward compatible)
```ts
ChapterDef.start.y?: number                  // elevated start: humans up there, pups on the ground below
ChapterDef.pups?: ClassId[]                  // pup kits by rank (featured first)
reach.params.minY? / maxY?                   // feet at least minY (humans; waived bot-only; grace) · zone ceiling
interact.params.minY? / item?                // the same floor · the object shown at the point (E takes it)
ChapterStep.vehicles?: { vehicle: 'mower_kart'; x; z; yaw }[]   // parked when the step starts
ChapterStep.raise?: { x; z; yaw }[]          // barricade walls raised when the step completes
ChapterStep.rally?: { x; z; y? }             // where the squad regroups/respawns afterwards (y: humans only)
ADVENTURE_ITEM_IDS = ['catnip', 'tennis_ball']   // append only
STRICT_GRACE_SECONDS = 120, GRACE_BARK, BARRICADE_HP = 650, VEHICLE_REPARK_SECONDS = 4
```

### Runner (`src/sim/adventure/`)
- **`props.ts` (new).**
  - `kitPups` runs at chapter load, and on a pup's first spawn event mid-chapter. It uses S1's `swapKit`, full hp, and
    drops `e.ai` so the brain rebuilds with the kit's profile (bomber, guard, raider…).
  - Vehicles: `parkStepVehicles` uses V1's `spawnKart`, team Corgis. `reparkVehicles` parks a wrecked kart again
    while its step or a later one is live. `clearVehicles` dismounts the rider and drops the collider handle, like
    V1's destroy.
  - Barricades: `raiseBarricade` builds C2's `spawnAbilityEntity` + barrier collider, and does the push-out.
    `standingBarricades` and `clearBarricades` handle checkpoints and restarts.
  - The pop-up `ability squeak_barrier` event carries **the wall's id**, like X1's kiosk vend. The human's HUD Q ring
    and avatar voice stay out of it.
- **`runner.ts`:**
  - the `minY`/`maxY` checks;
  - the grace, with the bark once at 120 s;
  - the interact item and its `pickup`;
  - vehicles (parked on enter, re-parked while live);
  - `raise` on step completion;
  - `rally`;
  - `anchorY`, which puts humans on elevated rally/start points while pups use the ground formation;
  - the beacon on the roof for `minY` steps;
  - checkpoints save the standing walls, and a restart re-raises them and re-parks the vehicles of steps
    `0..checkpoint`.
- **`spawns.ts`.** `spawnItem` takes an optional top height, so the ball sits on the roof.
- **`state.ts`.**
  - `AdventureState.anchorY`;
  - `CheckpointSnap.anchorY` / `barricades`;
  - runtime `vehicles` / `barricades` / `kitted`.

### Bots (`src/sim/ai/`)
- **Prop targeting.** Tactics' `propTarget` runs only in an adventure `destroy` step. It picks the prop when the
  bot's weapon can hurt it:
  - hitscan (not charged) on a prop that takes shots;
  - an explosive lob on one that takes full blasts.

  It also sets a standoff band: 0–18 m for hitscan, 6.5–24 m for lobs, which keeps them out of their own splash.
  The brain's `propShot` works like C2's `droneShot`. With no character target, it checks sight every 6 ticks, to
  just short of the prop's nearest face. It then aims (a ballistic lob at the foot for projectiles) and fires once
  lined up. In patrol the bot stands still while it has the prop in sight and in band, and backs off if closer than
  the band. Overwatch's charged rifle is excluded, as in `droneShot`.
- **Breaching.**
  - `chargeFits` measures to the prop's boxes, within X1's reach − 0.5 m, instead of to its anchor. A charge the bot
    left elsewhere no longer blocks the plant.
  - `approachProp` walks to the face nearest the bot, so the alley side stays the alley side.
  - Chapter 3's breach went from 32 s (a charge 2.8 m from the boards on its 30 s fuse) to 2.7 s.
- **Walk intent.** Sentry posts set `tac.walk`. The brain scales the move to walk speed and doesn't sprint.
- **Scope.** Nothing else in the brain changed. Outside an adventure destroy step `tac.prop` is always −1 (tested in
  a skirmish).

### 4.1 `src/host/room.ts › sendRoster()`: the scoreboard shows a pup's chapter kit

```ts
    const players: RosterEntry[] = [...this.players.values()].map((p) => ({
      pid: p.pid, name: p.name, team: p.team,
      // A2: adventure chapters re-kit their pups in the sim; show the kit the entity has

      entity: p.entity, bot: p.bot,
      kills: p.kills, deaths: p.deaths, score: p.score, ping: p.ping,
    }));
```
Without it, the scoreboard lists e.g. "Pup 2 · Infiltrator" while that pup plays a Breacher. That is cosmetic only;
the avatars (snapshot `cls`) are right.

## 6. Known issues
- **Bots don't drive, fly, glide on purpose or climb.**
  - The `vehicle`, `airborne` and `minY` rules are waived for a bot-only squad (A1's rule, extended to `minY`).
  - So bot-only runs don't exercise the kart, plane, glide or perch beats; the human stand-in tests do.
  - R1's `planeAutopilot` could fly a pup pilot later: a system between AI (100) and plane-step (191) that writes its
    command for a seated bot.
- **Pups escorting a roof step wait in the zone below** while a human climbs: inside the garage under the chapter 4
  perch, or under the chapter 6 pad.
- **Chapter 6's glide needs a Skyraider.**
  - The picker gives it. Online co-op joiners bring their own kits, and without a Skyraider the step waits for the
    120 s grace.
  - The same holds for anyone who can't yet fly the plane or climb onto the shed.
- **The flyover zone completes ~10 m early.** It is r 14 around (47, 84), so it completes as the plane enters that
  airspace, about 10 m before it is over the eave. The data test needs walkable ground inside the radius, and the shed
  is a 26 × 16 m solid.
- **Chapter 5's difficulty for humans is unknown.**
  - Bot-only never wiped (0/6).
  - The barricades mostly deny sight lines rather than soak damage: in seed 1 they ended untouched, because cats path
    around them.
  - Humans certify fun; if it's soft, grow wave 3 or put the walls in the cats' firing lanes.
- **The Vac-Tank dominates chapter 6** (135–157 s of ~170–186 s bot-only). This is B1's balance with its squad
  scaling.
- **The kart can be wrecked** by the chasers. It is parked again at the roll-up door 4 s later, and V1 ejects the
  rider.
- **Until §4.1 lands,** the roster shows the pups' Room kits.
- **Intro captions headless.** A1's issue: they pace by frame time, so at 1–2 fps the intro panel stays up for ~30 s
  of wall time.
- **Chapter 2 sentries walk now.** This had no effect on the seed-1 bot runs (79.5 s either way); a flat-yard test
  proves top speed ≤ walk speed.

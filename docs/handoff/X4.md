# X4 handoff — one throwable per faction (Wave 9, arms-2)

**Status: done for integration; nothing committed or staged.** Corgis carry a **Squeaker Grenade**, cats a **Hairball
Bomb**: one at a time, every pet spawns with one, your own Ordnance Kiosk restocks you. The authority owns all of it
(carry, cooldowns, launch, flight, fuse, blast); the client draws it from snapshots, previews the arc with the
authority's own arc function, and adds a HUD slot, faction FX and audio.

**No protocol change, no gun number changed.** A thrown one is an `EntityKind.Projectile` snapshot entity; carrying
is `EFlag.Ordnance` (C9); restocks are `pickup` events. `WEAPONS`, `COMBAT_RULES` and `CLASSES` are byte-identical (a
content hash test), and so is the gun TTK table (a snapshot test).

The sim side is live once this lands: `combatSystems()` registers it, so every mode in `ORDNANCE_RULES.modes` hands
them out and G throws. The client side needs the `main.ts` and `fx/index.ts` snippets in §5. The bots need the brain
hook in §5.4.

## 2. The numbers, and why (game-design-psychology: a soft counter)
**The role:** flush a camper, punish a cluster, open a push. It must never replace the guns, and the target must be
able to read it and react (fairness: a telegraph plus time to act).

| Number | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Blast at the centre | **70** (both factions) | The frailest classes (Infiltrator, Overwatch: 90 hp) keep 20: never a one-shot, one more rifle burst finishes it. That is the "opener, not the kill". The Breacher's mortar shell does 25 + 85, so the throwable never out-explodes the explosives class. |
| Falloff | full inside 0.7 m, 20 % at the 4.0 m edge | By distance to the capsule: 0 m 70 · 1 m 64.9 · 2 m 47.9 · 3 m 31.0 · 4 m 14.0. A pair standing 2 m apart both lose ~half their health: clusters are punished, a lone target near the edge barely notices. |
| Radius | 4.0 m | Covers a "4 m cluster" (the AI's own definition) from its centre; just under the mortar's 4.2. |
| Knockback | 7 m/s (the mortar's 10) | Shoves a camper out of cover without launching anyone across the map. |
| Self-damage | 35 % (like the mortar) | A throw at your own feet costs 25 hp: you can take the risk. Friendly fire stays off, as for every explosive. |
| Fuse | **2.2 s** from the release | Flight is 0.6–1.7 s over 8–22 m, which leaves the target 0.5–1.6 s on the ground to react. The blink speeds up from every 0.45 s to every 0.07 s, and the danger ring shows the radius for the last 0.8 s. A sprinting pet clears 4 m in about 0.5 s. |
| Flight | 19 m/s, −16 m/s², a 15° lob | 24 m is the longest throw (a 45° lob). Looking at the horizon throws ~15 m, and looking down drops it at your feet. It is floatier than the mortar (−18) so the arc reads. |
| Carry | 1 | Grenade spam is the classic failure of the genre. One per life keeps it a decision. |
| Restock | 20 s after the throw, automatic at your own kiosk | Automatic because E already opens the kit picker at the kiosk (S1 flow untouched: one press, one meaning). The clock runs from the throw, so camping the kiosk gives at most one per 20 s: 70 damage per 20 s is under 5 % of a rifle's DPS. |
| Throw cooldown | 0.8 s | The abuse backstop (a restock right after a throw). |
| Emote lock | 1.0 s after a taunt | A taunting pet has its paws busy (the card's "not mid-emote"). |
| Bounce feel (the only faction difference) | Squeaker: restitution 0.36, grip 0.5, roll 4.5 m/s²<br>Hairball: restitution 0.12, grip 0.3, roll 9 m/s² | The squeaker lands and hops 3–4 m on (the bots aim it short); the hairball dies where it lands and rolls ~0.5 m. The same blast, fuse and flight. |

Who carries them: players and room bots. Never the PvE wave or chapter cats (`combat.pve`) or bosses, so the skirmish
difficulty band only changes on the players' side.

Modes: `yard-skirmish`, `team-deathmatch`, `core-rush`, `boss-rush` and `base-assault`. **Adventure is off**: its
chapters and par medals were tuned without throwables, and chapter 3's wall is an "explosives only" step. Adding
`'adventure'` to `ORDNANCE_RULES.modes` turns it on. Note that `base-assault` has no kiosk until G4a/the lead adds it
to `TERMINALS.ordnance_terminal.modes`. Until then there, spawn-with-one still works.

## 3. How it works
**Carry (805, after respawn 700 and match 800).**
- Every eligible character gets `ordCarry` on first sight, carrying one.
- A death clears the carry and the flag. A respawn gives a fresh one.
- A **match restart** (a new `MatchState` object) fizzles every throwable in flight (despawned, no blast) and refills
  everybody.
- `EFlag.Ordnance` is written here each tick.

**Throw (455, after weapons 400 and abilities 450).**
- A `Btn.Throw` **press** while carrying arms; the **release** throws, so hold-to-aim and a tap both work.
- Edges come from the system's own last-seen button state, not `e.prevButtons`. The Room's catch-up replays inputs
  movement-only and rewrites `prevButtons`, which would swallow a press or a release (tested through the Room; see
  §6.1).
- A press held across a death never throws the respawn one.
- `tryThrow` validates, in order: carrying, alive, not `Mounted`, not `Stunned`, the emote lock, the 0.8 s cooldown,
  `combatLive`.
- The launch is `throwLaunch(e.pos, e.yaw, e.pitch)`: the eye + 0.14 m up, 0.24 m to the right (the camera's shoulder),
  0.3 m forward; the view pitch + 15°, clamped −0.7…1.2 rad. Nothing from the client but yaw/pitch/buttons, and
  `sanitizeInput` strips anything else (tested through the Room guard).
- A hand pushed into a wall throws from where the arm is still in the open.
- Throwing ends spawn protection and breaks a fire-breaking cloak, like a shot. It makes no noise record (the throw path
  allocates nothing); the blast reports noise as every explosion does.

**Flight (455).**
- The entity comes from a per-sim pool (64). It is stepped by the shared `stepBody` once per tick, from the tick after
  the release.
- The contact query:
  - Rapier (static world + vehicles; the thrower's own barriers let it through);
  - the terrain collider's exact surface as a backstop (vertical rays can slip between heightfield or trimesh cells);
  - **enemy** capsules: it bounces off them, with no damage;
  - allies and the thrower are passed through.
- Contact rules:
  - above `settle` normal speed it bounces (tangential × grip, normal × restitution);
  - below it, it rolls (rolling friction);
  - on flat-ish ground it rests (`EFlag.Grounded`) and re-checks its support every 8 ticks, so a crate breaking under it
    makes it fall.
- At the fuse (132 ticks), `explode()` fires at the body + 0.15 m, with the shared blast. That covers LOS, falloff,
  knockback, the `explode` event, noise, and destructibles through the blast listeners.
- If combat isn't live (match over, warmup), it fizzles instead: no damage, no shove after the whistle.
- Leaving the world (kill plane or bounds) also fizzles it.
- **Snapshot:** `weapon = 100 + ORDNANCE_IDS index` (never a weapon index; `weaponByIndex` returns null for it),
  `ammo = fuse ticks left`, `seed = the thrower`, `team`/`species` = the thrower's, `flags & Grounded` = at rest.

**Restock (455).** `restockAtKiosk` applies when you:
- don't carry one;
- are alive and on foot;
- are past `restockAt` (throw + 20 s);
- stand at **your own** kiosk (`ownKioskFor` is `ordnanceTerminalFor`'s rule).

It gives one and emits `pickup` with the ordnance id. The HUD already says "Picked up squeaker grenade", with a chime,
a sparkle and YOINK.

## 4. Bots (`ordnance-ai.ts`, pure)
`planThrow(bot, enemies, allies, arcWorld)` says yes only when all of these hold:
- the bot carries one, is alive, on foot and not stunned;
- **≥ 2 perceived enemies** stand within 4 m of each other;
- their centre is **8–22 m** away;
- `solveThrow` (the preview's own arc, over the same `ArcWorld`) lands the throw. The squeaker's bounce/roll is
  corrected: the aim point moves short by the predicted roll until the **blast point** is within 1.5 m of the centre;
- the first landing is on open ground (normal up);
- ≥ 2 of them are inside 85 % of the radius with a clear line from the blast point;
- no ally (and not the bot itself) is within 5 m of it.

`ordnanceBotThink` sequences it:
- a re-plan every 30 ticks (staggered by id);
- on a plan, Throw is held for at least 12 ticks while the bot turns to the solved angles. That wind-up is the target's
  tell;
- the release comes on the exact angles once aligned (snapped after 45 ticks);
- then a 90-tick cooldown.

It is deterministic and never touches `sim.rng`. Cost: ~0.4–0.65 ms per plan attempt, and only when a cluster sits in
the band; ≤ 1 attempt per bot per 0.5 s.

Tested: a pair 14 m out (yes, blast by them), a lone enemy, too close, too far, an ally in it, no throwable, in a
vehicle (all no), and a closed shed (no). End to end: a bot driven by the snippet below throws a real grenade that hurts
both cats and no corgi.

### 5.3 `src/client/main.ts`
```ts
import { createOrdnanceView, type OrdnanceAim } from './fx/ordnance-view';
import { createOrdnanceAudio } from './audio/presets-ordnance';
import { createOrdnanceHud } from './ui/ordnance-hud';
// (if not imported yet: Btn from '../shared/input', EFlag + type SpeciesId from '../shared/types', type EntityState from '../shared/protocol')

// after `const fx = createFx(...)`:
const ordAudio = createOrdnanceAudio(audio.engine);
const ordView = createOrdnanceView(ctx.scene, { world: worldData, onCue: (c) => ordAudio.cue(c) });
const ordAim: OrdnanceAim = { holding: false, yaw: 0, pitch: 0 };
let ordButtons = 0;
// after `const hud = createHud(ui, ...)`:
const ordHud = createOrdnanceHud(document.getElementById('cvc-hud') ?? ui);

// applySettings(st): next to fx.setQuality(st.quality)
ordView.setQuality(st.quality);

// the bus 'game' handler, next to fx.onGameEvent(ev):
ordView.onGameEvent(ev);

// the input loop: replace the sample line so the throw is heard on release (the authority throws on the same edge)
if (net?.connected && !net.awaitingSpawn) {
  const cmd = input.sample(++seq, TICK_DT);
  cmd.rt = Math.max(0, Math.round(net.renderTime(now) * net.tickHz));
  const me = ordLocal; // last frame's local state (see below)
  if ((ordButtons & Btn.Throw) && !(cmd.buttons & Btn.Throw) && me && (me.flags & EFlag.Ordnance) && !(me.flags & (EFlag.Dead | EFlag.Mounted | EFlag.Stunned)))
    ordView.localThrow(me.species as SpeciesId, me.x, me.y + 1.2, me.z);
  ordButtons = cmd.buttons;
  net.pushInput(cmd);
}

// each frame, after `const local = states.get(localId) ?? null` and before ctx.render():
ordLocal = local;
ordAim.holding = (ordButtons & Btn.Throw) !== 0; ordAim.yaw = input.yaw; ordAim.pitch = input.pitch;
ordView.update(pdt, states, localId, ordAim);
ordHud.update(local, now / 1000);
// (declare next to ordAim: `let ordLocal: EntityState | null = null;`)
```
- The preview draws only while the local pet holds G carrying one, alive, on foot and not stunned. It uses the pet's
  predicted feet (prediction is bit-exact with the authority) and the live view angles, i.e. the numbers the release
  input will carry.
- The release check reads last frame's local state; one frame of lag on the local release sound is fine.
- A throw pose on the avatar isn't in the Avatar contract (`views/**` is off limits for me). `views.get(id)?.avatar.trigger('fire')`
  on the local release is a stand-in if you want one.

### 5.4 Bots — the brain/tactics hook (G4b owns `brain.ts`/`tactics.ts`; paste at INT9)
```ts
// brain.ts imports
import { createOrdnanceBot, makeIntent, ordnanceBotThink, type OrdnanceBot, type OrdnanceIntent } from './ordnance-ai';
import { ordnanceArcWorld } from '../combat';
// AiState: add
  ord: OrdnanceBot; ordIntent: OrdnanceIntent; seen: SimEntity[];
// createBrain(): add  ord: createOrdnanceBot(0), ordIntent: makeIntent(), seen: [],
//   (and in applyArchetype / ensureBrain once the entity is known: ai.ord = createOrdnanceBot(e.id) — staggers re-plans)
// perceive(): collect every enemy that passes the visibility test, not only the best —
//   at the top: ai.seen.length = 0;   right after the worldLineClear check passes: ai.seen.push(t);
// think(): just before "// ---- write the input", room bots only (never wave/chapter cats, never in adventure):
  if (!e.combat?.pve && roomModeOf(sim) !== 'adventure') {
    const allies = ctx.chars; // planThrow skips enemies and the bot itself in this list
    const oi = ordnanceBotThink(ai.ord, e, sim.tick, ai.yaw, ai.pitch, ai.seen, allies, ordnanceArcWorld(sim), ai.ordIntent);
    if (oi.active) {
      if (oi.buttons) turnToward(ai, a, oi.yaw, oi.pitch, dt, 1.5); // the wind-up: turn to the throw, G held
      else { ai.yaw = oi.yaw; ai.pitch = oi.pitch; }                   // the release: exactly the solved angles
      buttons = (buttons & ~(Btn.Fire | Btn.Aim)) | oi.buttons;
    }
  }
```
- `planThrow` filters by team itself: enemies of the bot's team and allies of the other team are skipped, so passing
  `ctx.chars` as `allies` is safe. Pass the **perceived** enemies (`ai.seen`) as `enemies`: throwing at pets it cannot
  see would be wallhacking.
- `roomModeOf` is tactics.ts's helper; a mode check against `ORDNANCE_RULES.modes` is equivalent.
- `perceive` runs every 6 ticks, so `ai.seen` is at most 0.1 s old, which is fine for a 0.5 s re-plan.

### 5.5 Recommended one-liners (lead-owned files)
- **`src/host/room.ts` `COMBAT_BUTTONS`: add `Btn.Throw`.** Catch-up extras then stop at a Throw change, like at a fire
  press. My system already survives a lost press or a lost release (own edge tracking, tested). What it cannot see is a
  whole one-tick tap that lands inside a catch-up batch.
- **`src/sim/vehicles/common.ts` `blastDamageOf` and `combat/ability-entities.ts` `blastProfile`:** today a throwable's

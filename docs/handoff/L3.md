## 1. M3, atmosphere

### Floodlights from `WorldData.floodlights` (the S4 rig, generic)
- `buildTheLot` fills `floodlights`: one per tower, from the mean of its four sodium lenses to the ground point it aims
  at. It is seed-stable and tested against the lens lamps.
  - The two heap towers now aim at the heap top in front of the scaffold, (26, 106) and (44, 106), instead of the
    scaffold itself. The fake pool lies on the ground it lights, and the real spot cone still covers the deck.
- `battle-dressing.ts` gets its list from **`floodlightSpecs(data)`**:
  - `WorldData.floodlights` when a map fills it;
  - otherwise the E4 battle layout's floods, exactly as before.

  No `the_lot` string checks anywhere. A map's towers need no battle layout (the dressing no longer returns early
  without one; cloth and clutter still need the layout).
- **The West Yard keeps its E4 source.** Its spec list is test-equal to the old one (same targets, same pools, no
  intensity/range: the style's defaults), and its draws and triangles are identical at three bookmarks (§4).
- **Far-throw towers.** The style's floodlight is tuned for E4's 10–13 m pole throws; The Lot's towers throw 33–39 m.
  The adapter treats a field light as the same lamp moved `s = throw / 12 m` times farther:
  - `range × s`;
  - `intensity × s² × FLOOD_TOWER_GAIN`;
  - pool radius `× s` (max 22 m), shrunk until the disc never floats more than 0.6 m over the ground within 75 % of its
    radius. That is what keeps the heap pools from overhanging the heap's north face.

  The rig's look code (`floodlights.js`) is untouched.
- **The gain (0.12) was found on one frozen frame.** At gain 1 and at 0.45, the pit floor clipped to sodium white:
  pit luma was 57.0 at both, because the toon ramp's top band saturates. So I scaled the real lights ×1 / ×0.25 / ×0.1
  / off on the same frame (`artifacts/l3/scratch/pit-ab.png`). 0.12 reads as a warm pool with a visible falloff, and the
  footings keep their colour.

### Crane lamps (glow through the lamp system, no real lights)
- `towerCrane()` pushes `WorldData.lamps`:
  - `laserRed` aviation lights at the jib tip and the apex, two crossed 1.1 m tubes each, so they read from any side;
  - five warm `lampTube` tubes over the cab window. They join the container tubes' instanced draw.
- Cost: **+1 draw** (the red group).

### Rain by default: a per-map weather bias (data on the world)
- `weather.ts`: every schedule function (`weatherAt`, `weatherParamsAt`, `findWeather`, strikes, sprinklers) now takes a
  **`WeatherKey`**, which is a bare seed (the default schedule, bit-for-bit unchanged) or the world itself
  (`{ seed, weatherBias }`).
- **`LOT_WEATHER`** (`lot/layout.ts`), deterministic per seed. Minutes per 13-minute cycle:

  | Segment | Minutes |
  |---|---|
  | overcast (cycle 0 only) | 1.3–1.8 |
  | rain | 2–3.5 |
  | storm | 1.5–2.5 (always in cycle 0, 85 % later) |
  | rain | the rest of the cycle |
  | clearing | 1–1.5 |
  | clear | 1–2 |
  | overcast | 0.8–1.5 |

  Measured over 8 cycles: wet 78 %, storm 15 %; the West Yard is wet 34 %.
- **The authority reads it** (`sim/world/env.ts`, `systems.ts` pass the world instead of its seed): wet mud is slippery
  and AI sight is ×0.85 in rain. **Prediction** reads it through the same `stepWorldEffects(data)` on the client's
  own world copy (test: authority slip == prediction slip).
- **The West Yard is unchanged:** a golden SHA-256 of its cycles, samples and strikes taken on HEAD 38db372 is
  asserted.
- **Why the opening is overcast.** Rain from tick 0 made the 60 s soak skirmish miss its match: wave 3 at 14:3,
  against HEAD's 21:2 at 53 s. The same build with only the bias switched off passed (21:1 at 51 s). A dry first
  minute, as on the default schedule, fixed it: the soak skirmish finishes 21:3 at 51 s.
- `WorldData` has no `weatherBias` field yet. `buildTheLot` returns `LotWorld = WorldData & { weatherBias }` (snippet
  S-1 adds the optional field). The world view still reads the bare seed until S-2, so in the game the client shows the
  default weather while the authority rains. That is visual only; prediction is already consistent. `labs/lot.ts`
  feeds the view the world's schedule itself, so the lab shows it now.

### Muddy ditch water, value lift, calmer rims
- **Water.** The four `ditch_*` zones carry `tint: 'mud'`. In `createWaterView`, a tint recolours the default water
  material: every colour it produces becomes the tint scaled by that colour's brightness relative to the default
  shallow water. So the shallows are exactly 'mud', deeper water is darker, and the ripples and shore foam are lighter
  mud. Untinted zones (the pond, the pool) are untouched.
  - From the ditch it reads as tan muddy water catching the sky; from the overview as dark brown strips.
- **Value lift.** At dusk, the bags in Container Canyon read near-black.
  - Cement-bag walls: sandbag/canvas → **pale paper sacks** (`bannerRag`, a `canvas` one in three).
  - The east container: khaki → **`hull`** (warm greige).

  The bags now read as pale sacks, and the container reads pale from the overview. Its interior stays dark: it is an
  enclosed bunker lit only by its glow tube.
- **Rims.** The pale gravel rims round the pit and trenches are narrower, and broken into ~3 m spoil patches by a
  second noise. The pit and trench shapes still read from the overview, without the continuous white outline.

### Fog scaled by the map's size (lead snippet S-4)
In rain, now the Lot's default, the far third of the lot was lost from the overview, houses included (luma 70,
contrast 21). `fogScale = 118 / (edge + 1)` multiplies the sky's fog density, so the fog's distances scale with the
map. It is **exactly 1.0 on the West Yard** and 0.75 on The Lot. It brings back the houses, the hoarding line and the
lit pit in rain (contrast 21.3 → 24.1; `artifacts/l3/scratch/fog-cmp.png`). The after gallery below includes S-2 and
S-4, prototyped in the scratch snapshot.

### Lanes: `src/sim/ai/lanes.ts` (new, pure) + a 3-line hook in `tactics.ts`
- **The data.** `LOT_LANES` (`lot/layout.ts`) lists five lanes from the corgi end to the cat end, as ground waypoints,
  some with a linger (a lane's hot spot). Cats walk the lanes backwards.

  | Lane | Weight | Linger |
  |---|---|---|
  | Canyon (between the containers) | 0.23 | 4 s mid-canyon |
  | Canyon W (through the west container) | 0.16 | 3 s inside it |
  | the Mud (the middle causeway) | 0.17 | 2 s either side of the ditch |
  | Pipes W (through the west tunnel) | 0.24 | 2 s at its north mouth, 4 s in its middle |
  | Pipes E (through the east tunnel) | 0.20 | 2 s at its north mouth, 3 s in its middle |

- **The pick.** Each life (`e.respawnTick`), a bot hashes (world seed, bot id, life) into a weighted pick, with no RNG
  draw from the sim.
- **Walking it:**
  - it walks the lane waypoint by waypoint and lingers at the hot spots;
  - a waypoint it is already past gets skipped;
  - one with no progress for 12 s gets skipped; the third skip in a row ends lanes for that life.
- **The sweep back.** At the far end, a bot **sweeps back home along the next pick** (leg 1, 2, …). It is not released to
  hunt: in the first version, bots released at the far end drifted back into the open middle. After other lanes' bot
  changes that gave canyon/mud 0.24 in the 22:53 gate run, and the sweep-back fixed it.
- **Who walks lanes:** TDM room bots only (`LANE_MODES`). Not marksmen, because `autoPerch` runs only while a bot has
  no tactics goal: a lane goal would empty the perches. Not PvE waves, not player stand-ins, not bots holding a perch.
  No lanes on maps without them: the West Yard is untouched, which is tested.
- **The hook** (`tactics.ts`, my only edit there):
  - `updateObjectiveGoal` keeps its 0.5 s throttle, calls the body (now `objectiveGoal`, otherwise unchanged), and then:
    `if (!t.goal) laneGoal(sim, e, t);`
  - The goal union gains `'lane'`.
  - The brain walks it like any patrol-mode tactics goal. Helping a human under fire is perception's alert mode, so it
    still comes first.
  - G4b's committed Base Assault line sits inside `objectiveGoal`, so base-assault bots never get a lane. Against HEAD
    13dc2ee the diff is my lines only.
- **Result** (14v14 TDM, 90 s live after 5 s, 4 seeds pooled; `lot-lanes.test.ts` gates both ratios at ≥ 0.25). The

## 8. Known issues and next
1. **S-2 is needed for the in-game look.** Until it lands, the client draws the default schedule on The Lot while the
   authority rains. Wet mud and AI sight are already authoritative, and prediction agrees.
2. **The budget margin is 42 draws** at 14v14 on The Lot (358 of 400 on 13dc2ee). Most of the step is the pickups. The clutter
   pass (M3 optional) should wait for that margin to be re-planned.
3. **Container interiors are dark.** They are enclosed, lit only by their glow tubes, and a light there would take one
   of the four real lights. A fake floor pool per tube would be a floodlight-rig feature: P4 or the style lane.
4. Core-rush on The Lot swings between lopsided and even pad results: 0:43 (score 87, F 61) on HEAD 8b171aa, 13:40
   (94) on 13dc2ee. It passes either way; the pads are the runtime `corePadSpots()` picks (W8).
5. **P4 is changing `sky.ts` in the working tree** (the dusk fill light, `rimI`). My gallery and budget snapshots are
   HEAD-based and do not include it. The dark bookmarks it targets include The Lot's pipe mouth and canyon (luma 24.7 and
   34.5 by default). S-4 touches other lines of `sky.ts` (the options type, the `baseFog` line): no overlap.
6. Tooling:

   - a batch of shots in one page carries the pipeline's adapted state from one bookmark to the next (overview luma
     52 → 56.5), so every gallery shot uses a fresh page;

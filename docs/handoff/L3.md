projectiles, damage/death/respawn/regen, bark blast + shadow cloak, cat/corgi bot AI with nav-grid A*, and the

seed (same seed ⇒ bit-identical soak results). No shared contract was changed.

### Combat rules (`COMBAT_RULES`)
Friendly fire off (shots/projectiles pass through teammates) · respawn 3 s at `sim.pickSpawn(team)` · spawn protection
1.5 s (ends when you fire) · regen after 5 s without damage at 6 % max hp/s · PvE corpses removed after 4 s · headshot
= hit point above 80 % of capsule height · eye = 87.5 % of capsule height (corgi 1.05 m, cat 1.10 m) · lag-comp
history 64 ticks, max rewind 200 ms.

### Modes (`src/sim/match/config.ts`)
- **yard-skirmish**: warmup 5 s → 5 waves (4 grunts · +3 kittens · +1 sniper +4 kittens · 5/2/1/4 · **FINAL** 6 grunts,
  2 snipers, 2 brutes, 6 kittens) × squad scale `clamp(0.6 + 0.2·corgis, 0.8, 1.8)`, ≤ 10 alive at once, batches of 3
  every 1.6 s from the cat spawns, 7 s intermissions. Corgis: +1 per cat kill, +5 per wave. Loss = the whole corgi squad
  down at once more than `wipeLives` (2) times — generous, a solo player can fall twice. Room-slot cat bots join every
  wave and count toward its size (PvE spawns shrink by their number, grunts first, never below half the wave); they
  don't respawn mid-wave. **Human** cats always respawn and never hold a wave open. Result 12 s → restart.
  A 5-bot-corgi squad clears all 5 waves in ~4¼ min (full-config soak: Corgis win 101:74).
- **team-deathmatch**: warmup 5 s (no damage) → live 8 min, first to 30 kills → ended 10 s (winner, or draw) → restart
  (scores reset, everyone respawned with spawn protection).
- Any other `room.mode`: the match system stays idle (combat live, respawns on, Room's default MatchState).
- Overrides for tests/soaks: `sim.state.matchConfig = { skirmish?: Partial<SkirmishConfig>, tdm?: Partial<TdmConfig> }`.

## Integration notes (client / FX / HUD / netcode)

**Events (all existing `GameEvent` shapes, in emission order within a tick):**
- `fire` — once per shot. Hitscan: `x,y,z` = eye, `dx,dy,dz` = first pellet direction, `hx,hy,hz` = where it stopped
  (character, world, or max range), `hit` = entity or -1. Projectile weapons: `hit` = -1 and `h*` = muzzle point (the
  projectile entity carries the flight). Laser fires on trigger *release*.
- `hit` — one per target per shot (pellets aggregated), integer `dmg`, impact point, `crit` = head zone. Also for blast,
  bark and self damage (`src === dst` for self). Never emitted for blocked damage (friendly/protected/warmup).
- `death` — `by` = killer; a self-kill within 5 s of taking enemy damage credits that enemy; pure suicide → `by === id`.
- `explode` (`r` = radius, `by` = owner) · `ability` (bark: eye position; cloak: feet) · `reload` (reload start) ·
  `spawn` (character creation **and** every `respawnNow`: respawns *and* match-restart teleports — snap camera /
  interpolation for that entity, and re-seed the local camera yaw from the entity yaw) ·
  `score` `reason`: `kill` (pts 1), `wave` (pts 5), `win` (pts 0, team = winner), `reset` (pts 0, both teams, on restart).

**Flags:** `Firing` = shot within the last 150 ms (always visible in 30 Hz snapshots) **or** a charge weapon charging;
`Reloading` during reloads; `Invulnerable` during spawn protection; `Stealthed` while cloaked (render the owner
translucent, enemies nearly invisible); `Alerted` on bots investigating/fighting ("!" telegraph); `Aiming` comes from
movement (`Btn.Aim`) — **draw the laser-pointer beam for `laser_longshot` carriers with `Aiming|Firing`** (snipers hold
Aim ≥ 0.9–1.1 s before firing: that is their telegraph). Dead characters keep `Anim.Dead` and their corpse falls/slides
(knockback deaths skid); PvE corpses vanish after 4 s (`gone`).

**Snapshots:** `weapon` (index into `WEAPON_IDS`) and `ammo` are set every tick. Projectiles are `EntityKind.Projectile`
entities (`weapon` = launcher index, `seed` = owner id, velocity in `vx/vy/vz`, yaw/pitch face travel); they get fresh
ids (never reused). PvE cat look: species Cat + weapon tells the breed (squeaker = tabby grunt, laser = Siamese,
sprinkler with `maxHp` 320 = chonk brute, claw_swipe = kitten).

**Aim:** humans aim through the over-the-shoulder camera. For entities with `ownerPid` (human slots) the authority
traces the crosshair ray from `pivot + AIM_RAY` (1.25 m up, shoulder 0.55 hip / 0.75 aiming, along `viewDir`) and fires
from the eye toward that point, so hits land under the crosshair. **Keep `src/client/camera/third-person.ts` in sync
with `AIM_RAY`** (import it). Recoil is presentation data (`WeaponDef.recoil`): the client kicks its own camera; that
changes the next input's pitch, so recoil is real but client-authored.

**Lag compensation:** `InputCmd.rt` must be the server tick label of the snapshot state the client was rendering
(what `main.ts` computes today). Rewinds are clamped to 200 ms (`COMBAT_RULES.maxRewind`); at 150 ms RTT + 100 ms
interpolation the oldest shots exceed it and get partial compensation — raise to 0.25 s if L4's matrix wants it.

**Room:** bots need no Room support (AI drives every `EntityKind.Bot`). Recommended fills: `yard-skirmish` `[2,0]` or
`[3,0]` (corgi allies), `team-deathmatch` `[4,4]`. With the current server/worker default (`0,4`) the 4 room cats
fight in every skirmish wave in place of PvE spawns (at most half of each wave) — it works, but a solo player then
faces team-fill-accuracy cats instead of the looser PvE grunts. The Room's roster K/D is not reset on match
restart; watch for `score` `reason:'reset'` if you want it to be.

**HUD:** `match.objective` is ready-to-show text ("Wave 2/5 — 7 cats left", "Wave 2 cleared! Wave 3 in 5",
"FINAL WAVE (5/5) — …", "Squad down! 1 retry left · …", "Corgis win 30–24!"); `timeLeft` counts the current step
(warmup, intermission, TDM clock, result hold; 0 during a skirmish wave).

## Tuning notes
- Rifle: first-shot accurate, 1.2° hip is reliable ≤ 12 m, ADS (0.35°) rewarded at range; bursts stay tight because
  bloom recovers in ~0.2 s. Headshots (×1.6) cut TTK to 0.4 s.
- Bots are deliberately beatable: 0.28–0.45 s reaction, 6° initial error settling to 0.75° over ~0.7 s (+ extra error
  for lateral target speed, ×1.8 for lobbed projectiles), burst fire with pauses, ~45–50 % hitscan accuracy in soaks.
  PvE grunts are a notch looser (co-op fodder). Knobs: `ARCHETYPES` (reaction, aimErr*, burst*, retreatHp, telegraph).
- Mirrored 4v4 bot TDM is species-balanced (116–120 vs 120–125 kills over 4×3 min). By class (bots): Overwatch
  1.4–2.0 k/d, Breacher ~1.4, Assault ~1.0, Warden 0.1–0.4 (see known issues).
- Skirmish with 3 bot corgis reaches wave 4–5; humans should clear it. `wipeLives`, `scale*`, `maxAlive` are the
  difficulty knobs.

## Known issues / next
- **Movement cost (lead/L2):** Rapier's KCC on the West Yard heightfield costs ~0.13 ms per character per tick (8× the
  flat slab; terrain-only 1.0 ms vs props-only 0.3 ms for 8 characters). 16+ characters alone approach the 3 ms tick
  budget. Try a coarser/chunked heightfield or check the `FIX_INTERNAL_EDGES` cost.
- **Heightfield vertical rays (L2/all):** straight-down Rapier rays miss the heightfield along grid lines (23 % on a
  0.5 m lattice). L3 never relies on them (projectiles have a `height()` backstop); client/other lanes should avoid them.
- Warden (shotgun) bots are weak in the open yard — they die closing distance; needs a cover-hopping approach.
- Data-only abilities: spotter_drone, dig_charge, squeak_barrier, ear_glide (fully specified in `abilities.ts`).
- No bot vertical navigation yet: decks/roofs are obstacles on the 2D grid (bots can still be launched onto them by
  jump pads and walk off). Maine Coon boss not started (Wave 2).
- Snapshot bandwidth in a full skirmish (17 characters + projectiles) measured ~42 KB/s JSON for one client — at the
  40 KB/s budget (L4).
- Nav grid build is ~110 ms cold / ~40 ms warm, once per world per process, on the first AI tick (a one-time hitch at
  match start in the worker).

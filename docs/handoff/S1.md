## 1. What players get
- **Ordnance Kiosk** (one per team base, placed at runtime near the spawns, about 9 m from the Kart-O-Matic):
  - It is a chunky toy vending cabinet with a wooden ammo-crate header.
  - Its screen has six class tiles with inked class symbols.
  - A giant tennis ball sits on top, with crossed toy bones behind it.
  - A painted "stand here" ring marks the ground in front of it.
  - Press **E** at your own team's kiosk to open a comic kit picker (six cards; press **1–6**). The pick swaps your kit **in place**: same spot, same health *fraction*, new weapon (fresh magazine), ability, speeds and max hp. There is a 1 s cooldown.
- **Upgrade Cores**. There are four contested spots, each within ±25 % of equal distance from both bases:
  - under the teal lawn chair;
  - under the orange lawn chair;
  - on the big tree's limb perch;
  - on top of the slide tower.

  How they spawn and refill:
  - Spots light up 15 s after the match goes live, staggered 6 s apart. The first four cores are one of each type, shuffled.
  - After a pickup, a spot refills in 35 s. The next core is rolled immediately, never the same type twice in a row at one spot. It shows as a dim ghost that grows while 12 pips count down.

  | Core | Effect | Length |
  |---|---|---|
  | Overclock (red) | +20 % fire rate | 20 s |
  | Thick Fur (gold) | +30 % max hp as a shield | 25 s |
  | Zoomies+ (lime) | +15 % move speed | 20 s |
  | Squeaky Clean (cyan) | ability cooldowns −50 % | 20 s |

  Buffs end on expiry, on death, at match end and on restart. They never carry across matches.
- **Golden Kibble**: 20 golden bone biscuits per map. Each is worth +25 roster score and comes back for everyone 60 s after it is taken. Where they are:
  - Easy, near the bases: the dog bowl, the frog statue, the swing seat.
  - Parkour: the deck railing, the doghouse ridge, the AC unit, the cat tree top, the shed roof ridge by the plank bridge, the cardboard fort, the bean-teepee crown, the Garden trellis bridge, the gnome watchtower.
  - Air: high above the trampoline (bounce through it).
  - Secrets: behind the firewood through the west dig hole, on the tarp through the east fence gap, over the rubber duck in the kiddie pool, under the birdbath on the mound.
- **Squeaker mission** (yard-skirmish, 3 steps, runs during the waves):
  1. Grab the Squeaker from the shed. It sits behind the cat spawns; Interact.
  2. Hold the trampoline for 20 s. Progress pauses while any cat is inside the zone (CONTESTED) and drains at ½ speed when nobody holds it.
  3. Bark at the Cat Tree (Interact).

  Rewards per step:
  - a `bark` line;
  - a `score` event (reason `objective`) worth +5, +5, then +25 team points;
  - roster points for the completers;
  - the grab step also sends `pickup` with the item `squeaker`.

  The mission card shows ✓ / ▶ / ○ per step, the hold progress bar and "CONTESTED!".

## 3. Sim (order 150, after V1's vehicle-interact in the same slot)
- **Gating.** The layer runs when `sim.state.room.mode` is in `TERMINALS.ordnance_terminal.modes` (`yard-skirmish`, `team-deathmatch`, `boss-rush`). The objective chain runs only for the chain's own mode (`yard-skirmish`; `boss-rush` counts as skirmish). Tests and labs can override it with `sim.state.interactConfig = { auto, terminals, cores, kibble, objectives, layout, chain, coreFirstSpawn, coreStagger, coreRespawn, kibbleRespawn }`. Net and other lanes' test rooms (`test`, `net-*` modes) get nothing.
- **Tick 0.** It places the kiosks, pickups and beacon:
  - `findOrdnanceSite` does a ring search around the team's spawn centroid for a flat, clear, dry spot.
  - The spot stays clear of spawns, jump pads, the team's Kart-O-Matic + kart pad (`kartKeepOut`), pickup spots and objective points.
  - Site searches are pure and cached per WorldData.
  - The kiosk is a World-layer cuboid (1.6 × 2.5 × 1.1 m) that matches the model.
- **Match phases.** Match modes count as `warmup` until `sim.state.match` exists.
  - `live`: the cores are scheduled and the chain runs.
  - `ended`: buffs end, cores go dark, the chain hides.
  - Restart: a new MatchState object, or ended → other. Everything resets: kibble returns, a new core deal, a fresh chain.
- **Pickups** are positional and authority-only:
  - Each tick, the closest eligible character whose capsule touches the pickup takes it (`touchesPickup`).
  - Eligible means alive, not a PvE wave cat, not a boss.
  - There is no message and no input that can request a pickup.
  - They emit `pickup { id: collector, item }` and queue roster credit (kibble 25, cores 10).
- **Buffs** reuse the stats other lanes already read:
  - `moveSpeed` scales the entity's own `char.move` copy; the saved values are assigned back on expiry, bit-exact.
  - `maxHp` adds a bonus to `health.max` and heals by the same amount; it is removed and clamped on expiry.
  - `fireRate` and `abilityCooldown` pre-drain `wpn.cooldown` / `abil.cooldown`, and only while they are > 0. This runs before weapons (400) and abilities (450), so the combat lane's carry logic is untouched.
  - A kit swap suspends the buffs, rebuilds the kit and re-applies them.
- **Cost** (full default systems, TDM 4v4 bots, 25 s): **0.022 ms/tick** steady. Placement costs **88–106 ms once** on the first sim of the process (cached after that).

## 4. Room rules for class and team choices (the lead's request, verifier P1)
Rule by situation:

| Situation | `{t:'class'}` | `{t:'team'}` |
|---|---|---|
| At your team's Ordnance Kiosk (≤ 2.6 m, alive, not seated) | **In place** via `trySwapKit`: same entity, spot, velocity and hp fraction. Kiosk cooldown 1 s (a second message in that second → `'ignored'`). No `welcome` is sent. | Deferred (see "anywhere else") |
| At a spawn point (≤ 6 m, `DEPLOY_RADIUS`), alive, **full health** | Respawn as before. This is the deploy-menu path: nothing to gain there. Uses the 1 s `switchAllowed`. | Respawn on the new team as before |
| Anywhere else, or dead | Stored as `PlayerSlot.pendingCls`. Applied **in place on the next respawn** (the entity's `spawn` event, i.e. after the normal 3 s timer or a match restart), with full hp. | Stored as `pendingTeam`. Applied on the next respawn with `respawnAs` (a new body at the new team's spawn). While dead it simply rides the respawn timer. |

- Why a deploy exception: without it, the offline menu's PLAY (which sends `class` right after spawning) would change nothing until your first death.
- Roster credits: after each tick, `drainRosterCredits(sim)` adds kibble, core and objective points to `p.score`. Restarts clear them with the existing `resetStats`.
- Existing menu-flow quirk (not new, in the lead's `main.ts`): `play()` sends `class` and then `team` in the same tick, so the 1 s rate limit drops the team message when both are sent. Send the team first, or once.

### 6.3 `MatchState` fold (objective text + points), in `src/sim/match/index.ts`
Put this at the end of `matchSystem.update`, after `updateSkirmish` / `updateTdm`:
```ts
import { objectiveState, takeObjectiveScore, foldObjectiveText } from '../interact';
const ms = sim.state.match as MatchState | undefined;
if (ms) {
  const [c, k] = takeObjectiveScore(sim);           // objective points not yet in the score (the 'score' events were already emitted)
  ms.score[0] += c; ms.score[1] += k;
  if (ms.phase === 'live') ms.objective = foldObjectiveText(ms.objective, objectiveState(sim)); // "Wave 2/5 — 6 cats left · ▶ Hold the trampoline 12/20s (2/3)"
}
```
- `foldObjectiveText` is idempotent. It strips an older fold first, because some skirmish ticks return without rewriting the text.
- The lead's new client helper `objectiveForTeam()` (`src/client/ui/objective.ts`) matches its regexes against the whole line. After the fold, its `— N cats left$` pattern no longer matches. Split on `' · ▶ '` first, map the base, then re-append (the Squeaker step is corgi-only; the mission card already hides for cats).
- `sim.state.objective` (`ObjectiveState`) has:
  - `step`, `total`, `id`, `text` ('' = nothing to show), `progress`, `contested`, the target `x, y, z`;
  - `pendingScore`, the running `score`, and `doneTimer`.
- Optional wave-flow hook: award `waveBonus` or trigger a set piece on `objectiveState(sim)?.step === total`.

### 6.4 HUD / prediction notes (need small contract or lane changes)
- **Buff state is not in snapshots.** The prompts' `BuffTracker` rebuilds buffs from `pickup` events plus `PICKUPS` durations, and clears them on death, restart or win. Late joiners do not see buffs that are already running.
  - Proposal A: an `EFlag` bit per core type.
  - Proposal B: a `buffs` bitmask field on `EntityState`.
- **Zoomies+ and prediction.** `LocalPredictor` does not know the +15 % speed, so the local player gets small continuous reconciliations during the buff: about 3 cm per snapshot at run speed, smoothed.
  - Fix after proposal A or B: the predictor multiplies `char.move` walk/run/sprint by `PICKUPS.zoomies_plus.buff.moveSpeed` while the bit is set.
- **Squeaky Clean and the HUD's Q ring.** The ring uses a fixed cooldown estimate. Pass `HudModel.ability.cooldownTotal × 0.5` while the local player has the buff (`prompts` BuffTracker or proposal A).
- The HUD already toasts `pickup` ("Picked up golden kibble / overclock / squeaker") and non-kill `score`. FX and audio already play the pickup sparkle and chime, and a ring plus whoomp for `kit_swap`.

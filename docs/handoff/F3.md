### 1.2 The fix, and why this one

**Base Assault on the West Yard paths without the thin pass** (`src/sim/ai/nav.ts`):
- `thinPassFor(sim)` is false only when the room's mode is `base-assault` and the world is the West Yard.
- The shared grid cache is keyed by that flag too, so a TDM room and a Base Assault room in one process never share a
  grid.
- The Lot, every other West Yard mode, the adventure and roomless sims keep N3's pass.
- The Base Assault pilot stays, as N3 shipped it.

It is the only single change with a measured fair result:

| Change | Result | Kept |
|---|---|---|
| Drop the pilot | 62 % | Outside 40-60 %. |
| Restrict the pilot to the near side | 85 % | — |
| Keep only the hedgehogs' thin cells in West Yard Base Assault | 67 % | — |
| Drop the thin pass for West Yard Base Assault | **55 %** | Keeps the pilot and every other mode's thin nav. |

**The numbers that justify turning the thin pass off here:**
- **Balance.**
  - Base, seeds 1-40: **18 : 15** (55 %), 4 unfinished, 3 same-tick draws. Captures 104 : 99 (51.2 %).
  - Mirror, seeds 21-40 (the corgis on the cats' side): **6 : 6**, 5 unfinished, 3 draws. Captures 52 : 56 (48.1 %).
    On HEAD the corgis' side won 8 : 5.
- **The final tree plays exactly like the measured arm.** `final` gives match records identical, field for field, to
  the `oldnav` arm's on base seeds 1 and 21, mirror seed 31 (300 s each) and base seed 3 (90 s). So the 40 + 20 matches
  above are `final`'s.
- **The grid is exactly the old one.** A Base Assault room's grid on `final` (seed 1) equals the `oldnav` tree's cell
  for cell. HEAD's differs from it by exactly its 46 thin cells.
- **The Lot is untouched.** A 120 s Base Assault on seed 21 gives an identical record on HEAD and on `cD` (the same nav
  as `final`).
  - The Lot keeps the thin pass, and it has no hangar.
  - The unit test pins it: The Lot's Base Assault grid is its TDM grid.
- **The pins in Base Assault stay short without the pass**, because F1's flag move keeps the ball runs off the
  kart-gate hedgehog.
  - N3's `wy.mts` harness on `cD`, Base Assault seeds 1-10, 300 s each.
  - **Hedgehog pins: worst 2.5 s** (seed 5, a pup at the corgi-side hedgehog (−24.4, −49.4)), 7 episodes, 9 bot-s in
    10 matches. N3 measured the old nav at worst 1 s on seeds 6-15.

    mirror). The soak limit is 5 s.
- **The pilot still flies:** a flight in 10 of 10 matches (16 sorties: corgis 4, cats 12). No carrier sat in or headed

  - It often goes up later than on HEAD. First sorties on seeds 1-8: 34.5, 51.5, 57.7, 115.1, 196.7, 197.2, 211.0 and
    248.5 s (`cD/scratch/firstflight.mts`, run on `final`). N3's test seeds (1 and 6) sortied at 34.5 and 54.9 s

  - `ai-plane-ba` needed one narrow edit: its West Yard test flies seeds 2 and 6, where it flew 1 and 6. Its premise

## 2. P2-1: chapter 7's sentries are drawn at the range they see

**The choice: draw the real range; do not shrink the sight.** Arguments from A7's hand-back and from fairness:
- **A7 built the chapter on 45 m of sight** (A7 §4.2):
  - the approach stays ≥ 53.7 m from every post (45 m + a 5 m loop);
  - the pipes are unseeable;
  - the open crossings fit the Infiltrator's cloak;
  - the par (bots 122-146 s, the scripted player 102-132 s) is measured against it.
- **Cutting sight to 14 m would gut that design.** The approach, the pipes' purpose and the cloak plan would all become
  moot, and the chapter would need a new layout and a new par.
- **The sentries' sight is also their combat sight after the alarm.** A 14 m cat would be blind in the hold and the
  getaway.
- **A7 named this fix (S-3):** "a far arc at the archetype's sight range × `weatherSightMult`". What the player sees then
  matches the rule that judges them: a fair, readable threat instead of a hidden one.

**What changed:**
- `src/sim/ai/archetypes.ts`:
  - `archetypeForSnapshot(cls, maxHp)`: the archetype behind a cat's snapshot, by the same unique-hp premise as K3's
    `squadKitFor`;
  - `detectionRange(a, weatherSight)` = `a.sightRange × weatherSight`.
- `src/sim/ai/brain.ts`: `perceive` computes `sight` with `detectionRange`. It is the same multiplication, so bit-identical.
- `src/client/adventure/views.ts`:
  - each sentry cone gets a child mesh `sentry_sight`: 15 dashes on a unit-radius arc (band 1.2 %: 0.54 m at 45 m)
    across the archetype's `fovHalf`, scaled to `sentrySightRange(state, weatherSight)`;
  - it is dimmer than the fan (glow 0.8) and red when alerted, like the fan. One geometry per field of view, disposed
    with the views;
  - the 14 m fan stays (the Garden's grass reach);
  - `sync` takes an optional `weatherSight` (1 when absent).
- `src/client/main.ts`: one line, `advViews.sync(…, worldView.weather.sight)`. It is the same weather sample the world
  view renders and the weather audio hears, from the same schedule the authority reads.
- In clear weather a grunt's arc is at 45 m, a kitten's at 35 m, an alley raider's at 40 m. In a storm: 27, 21 and 24 m.

**Tests** (`tests/unit/f3-sentry-sight.test.ts`, 5 tests, ~3 s):
- chapter 7's stealth sentries are grunts and kittens, and its alarms add an alley raider;
- for each of the three, spawned as `spawnChapterCat` does and read back through `sim.toState` (what a client gets):
  - `archetypeForSnapshot` finds it;
  - in each of 5 weathers the arc's scale is exactly `detectionRange(ARCHETYPES[a], WEATHER_PARAMS[w].sight)`;
  - the geometry's outer edge is at that radius, dead ahead, and spans the archetype's field of view;
  - the arc turns red when alerted and goes with the cone;
- **the real brain**, on a flat yard, for the three archetypes: a pet 0.4 m inside the drawn radius is perceived (in
  `ai.seen`), and 0.4 m outside it is not. That is the proof that drawn = detected.
- The existing `adventure-client` view test passes unchanged (the arc is a child of the cone).

**Chapter 7 still gold:** `npx tsx tools/qa-adventure.mjs bots --chapters night_shift --seeds 11,12,13,14` on `trial`

## 3. P2-6: the container rain under full scale

**Cause (measured).** In a storm inside a container, the peaks come from the steel voice's click layer:
- single drops ring three high-Q panel modes, summed with the box drum;
- muting the pings takes the master from +6.1 to −5.4 dBFS, and muting the dense patter changes nothing;
- the site sub-bus's combat duck was linear to intensity 1, and a nearby rifle every 0.5 s reaches ~0.4, so the duck
  was only ~−2 dB there.

**Fix** (`src/client/audio/presets-site.ts`, `site-ambience.ts`; The Lot only, since the West Yard builds no site
ambience):
- **A soft ceiling on the steel voice.** The pings, drum and patter sum into a WaveShaper: unity to 0.8, then a tanh
  shoulder to 1.0, which is also its ceiling for anything louder. One node per steel loop (≤ 2 live).
  - Tuned from knee 0.3 to 0.8: the gentlest knee already takes every scene under −5 dBFS.
  - RMS moves −0.2 dB by a container and −1.8 dB inside one (the trim).
- **The combat duck is full from 0.35 intensity** (`AMB.combatFull`), linear from 0, with the same depth (−6 dB). The
  loops and the one-shots use it (`combatGain`, pure).

**Before → after**, `npx tsx tools/f3-audio.mjs` (new: Q5's harness, adds the mix into the limiter, fuse-tick headroom
and West Yard digests) on `head` and `final`:

# V1 handoff: Vehicles (Vehicle Terminal and Mower Kart)

**Status: done, ready to integrate.** The authoritative vehicle systems, the client views and the lab are all finished, and the proofs are green.

The feature follows the "terminals before vehicles" rule. Each team base gets a **Kart-O-Matic** terminal. The world data has no fixed spot for it, so the sim picks the spot at runtime. The terminal vends one **Mower Kart** for its own team. The corgi version is called the Mower Kart and the cat version is called the Purrmower.

The kart is an arcade vehicle with these features:
- throttle, brake and reverse;
- steering scaled by speed;
- grip and a handbrake drift that releases into a mini-turbo;
- a limited boost meter;
- jump pads, airtime and wall bounces;
- ramming damage against characters, armor that weapons can wear down, and an explosion when it is destroyed.

**No shared contract was changed.** Two things are left for you:
1. The wiring line in `src/sim/systems/index.ts`. I tried it and then reverted it, because it breaks one of L4's assertions. See the wiring section below.
2. The client wiring in `main.ts`, the camera and `EntityViews`.

## Sim systems (order)
| Order | System | Does |
|---|---|---|
| 150 | `vehicle-interact` | **Tick 0:** places terminals via `findTerminalSite` (see gating below). **Rider checks:** unseats riders who are dead, removed, or teleported more than 1.5 m (respawn, match restart, kill plane). **Interact edge:** dismount first, then mount, then vend. **Timers:** terminal cooldowns and flags. **Hands on the wheel:** masks the rider's `Fire`/`Aim`/`Ability`/`Reload`/`Melee`/`NextWeapon`/`Crouch` so combat never sees them. Fire or Ability honks the horn instead. |
| 190 | `kart-step` | Per kart: rider input → `stepKart`. Then: out of bounds or below `killY` wrecks the kart; kart-vs-kart impacts; crash and landing damage. Characters it touches are rammed or shoved (bosses are rammed but never shoved or launched). The rider is re-seated and the snapshot fields are written. A kart left empty for more than 40 s despawns quietly. |
| 560 | `vehicle-damage` | Hitscan `fire` endpoints and `explode` radii recorded this tick (see "Kart armor") hit kart bodies. Destruction happens here. Riders killed this tick are thrown out before corpse physics (600). |

**Gating:** terminals are placed when `sim.state.room.mode` is in `TERMINALS.kart_terminal.modes` (`yard-skirmish`, `team-deathmatch`). You can also force it either way with `sim.state.vehicleConfig = { autoTerminals: true | false }`, which is read on the first tick. Because of this, bare unit-test sims of other lanes (boss arena, combat, AI) never get kiosks.

**Controls while driving** (the `InputCmd` from any character):
- `mz` = throttle (forward), brake (backward) and reverse.
- `mx` = steer.
- **Jump** = handbrake / drift.
- **Sprint** = boost.
- **Fire or Ability** = horn. The horn is also a noise that bots hear, 30 m.
- **E** = get out.

The camera yaw does not steer; mouse look is free while driving.

**Rules** (all numbers in `VEHICLES.mower_kart`; server-side only):

**Terminal**
- A character within 2.5 m of the kiosk, on the kiosk's own team, can vend. Only one kart per terminal can be out at a time.
- After its kart is destroyed, the terminal cools down for 20 s. It re-arms after 3 s if the kart despawned unused.
- The kart pops out on the pad. If the pad is blocked, it uses the first clear spot on a 1.8 m ring around it.

**Mount and dismount**
- Mount range is 2.5 m, and the kart must be moving at 4 m/s or less. The seat must be free. Enemies may steal an empty kart.
- Dismounting tries spots in this order: the driver's left, then right, back, diagonals and front, each at 3 distances, then the kart roof. Every spot must pass all of these checks:
  - a Rapier capsule-clear test against world, terminals and karts;
  - the ground at the spot is at or above `height()`;
  - it is within the bounds;
  - no static wall lies between the seat and the spot.
- If no spot is clear, the rider stays seated.
- When the kart is destroyed, the rider is ejected and launched: 7 m/s up and 4 m/s outward.

**Ram**
- If the closing speed on a character is above 6 m/s, the character takes `22 + 6 × (v − 6)` damage through the combat lane's `applyDamage`. That is about 76 at 15 m/s and 94 when boosting. The hit is credited to the driver.
- The character is then launched with `knockback()`. Teammates are only bumped.
- Below 6 m/s, characters are shoved along with the kart's own controller. If a character is pinned against a wall, the kart backs off instead of swallowing them.

**Armor and destruction**
- 260 hp.
- Hitscan does ×0.6 damage to the body. The rider above the hood is still a normal capsule target, so headshots on drivers work.
- Blasts do full damage.
- Walls: an impact above 11 m/s costs 4 hp per m/s above that.
- Landings: an impact above 19 m/s costs 3 hp per m/s above that.
- Kart vs kart: both take ram damage.
- At 0 hp:
  - the rider is ejected;
  - the terminal starts its cooldown;
  - the body is removed;
  - the kart calls `explode()` from `src/sim/combat` with r 4.5, 45 damage and 11 m/s knockback.
- The explosion is credited to the last attacker within 10 s, otherwise the driver, otherwise the kart. It can chain into nearby karts.

## Snapshot conventions (existing `EntityState` fields)
| Kind | Field usage |
|---|---|
| Vehicle (`EntityKind.Vehicle`) | `x, y, z` = **ground point**: the wheels' contact point, which is the surface under the center while grounded. `yaw` = heading. `pitch` = body pitch (+ = nose up); derive roll from `height()` (the view does). `vx, vy, vz` = velocity. `hp/maxHp` = armor. `weapon` = **rider entity id** (-1 = empty). `ammo` = **boost meter 0–100**. `team` = the team that vended it; its colors stay even when stolen. **Flags:** `Busy` = seat taken, `Grounded`, `Sprinting` = boosting or mini-turbo, `Crouching` = drifting. `anim` = `Drive` when occupied. |
| Terminal (`EntityKind.Terminal`) | `x, y, z, yaw` = kiosk ground point; the screen faces `yaw`, and the pad is `padOffset` to its right. `weapon` = its active kart id (-1 none). `ammo` = whole seconds of cooldown left. `hp/maxHp` = cooldown progress seconds / total. `flags & Busy` = unavailable. |
| Rider | `flags & Mounted`, `anim = Drive`. Position = the seat, set every tick. `vx/vz` = the kart's velocity. `yaw/pitch` = the rider's view (the avatar's head follows the camera). `Grounded` mirrors the kart. |

**`cls` is always -1 on the wire for these kinds.** `Sim.toState` maps `e.cls` through `CLASS_IDS`, and non-characters have `cls: null`, so their content index cannot be sent. With one vehicle and one terminal type this is harmless: the client falls back to index 0 (`vehicleByIndex(s.cls) ?? VEHICLES.mower_kart`). **Contract proposal:** have `toState` emit a content index for non-character kinds, for example from `e.data.contentIndex`. That becomes necessary once a second vehicle type exists, such as the RC plane.

## Events (existing `GameEvent` shapes only)
All vehicle `ability` events carry **`id` = the vehicle's id, not the rider's**. That way the HUD's class-ability cooldown, which watches `ability` events with `id === localId`, is not triggered by driving. To find the rider, read the vehicle state's `weapon` field.

| Event | When |
|---|---|
| `spawn { id: kart }` | Kart vended. The FX sparkle plays at the pad. |
| `ability 'mount' / 'dismount'` | At the seat or at the exit spot. |
| `ability 'horn'` | Fire or Ability while seated. There is a 0.6 s cooldown. |
| `ability 'boost'` | Start of a meter boost or of a drift mini-turbo. |
| `jump { id: kart }` | Launched by a jump pad. |
| `land { id: kart, impact }` | Touchdown faster than 3 m/s. |
| `hit { dst: kart }` | Armor damage. `src` = the shooter, the driver (kart-vs-kart), or the kart itself (crash or fall). |
| `hit { dst: character, src: driver }` | Ram damage, emitted by `applyDamage`. `death.by` is the driver. |
| `explode` | Kart destroyed, emitted through the combat lane's `explode()`. |

2. **Room / prediction (L4):**
   - `Room.stepExtra` (input catch-up) runs `stepCharacter` on any living character. It must **skip `EFlag.Mounted` entities**. Today it would emit spurious `jump` events when the driver holds Jump (the handbrake). The rider is re-seated the next tick.
   - The client `LocalPredictor` must **not predict the local character while its state has `EFlag.Mounted`**. Render it from the interpolated state instead. It sits in the kart, and both are interpolated at the same render time.
   - Kart prediction is possible later with `stepKart`:
     - make a kart KCC with `createKartController(world)`;
     - build the kart body from `kartHullPoints(def)` at `groundPoint + halfHeight` and let the KCC settle it (`EntityState.y` is the ground point, not the collider);
     - replay `kartInputFrom(cmd)`.
3. **Views** (`main.ts`): all of the following in one place.
   ```ts

   const vehicles = createVehicleViews(ctx.scene, { world: worldData, camera: ctx.camera });
   // frame loop, next to views.sync(...):
   vehicles.sync(states, pdt);
   // camera: while the local character is Mounted, chase its kart
   const kart = local ? mountedVehicle(local, states) : null;
   if (kart) {
     const c = vehicleCameraFor(kart);
     input.yaw = followYaw(input.yaw, c.yaw, c.followRate, dt); // swing behind the kart (skip while the mouse is moving if preferred)
     focus.set(kart.x, kart.y, kart.z);
     cam.update(focus, input.yaw, input.pitch, false, dt, Math.hypot(kart.vx, kart.vz) /*, c */);
   }
   ```
   The camera rig (`src/client/camera/third-person.ts`) needs a vehicle mode: `update(..., vehicle?: VehicleCameraParams)`.
   - Use `c.height` in place of `AIM_RAY.pivotHeight`, `c.distance` in place of the 4.2 m base, `c.fov`, and `c.shoulder` (0, centered).
   - Ease these in over about 0.3 s so mounting and dismounting doesn't jump (MASTER_PLAN §7.4).
   - Suggested values: distance 4.4 m at rest → 6.2 m flat out; FOV 64° → 73°, +5° while boosting; pivot 1.5 m; rest pitch −0.2; follow rate 1.5 → 5 /s. Follow rate is 0 while reversing, so the camera never swings to the front.


## Known issues / next
- **Kart armor uses an event tap.** Weapons never target kart bodies, so kart damage is derived from the combat lane's `fire` endpoints and `explode` events by wrapping `sim.emit` once per Sim.
  - The approximations: a spread weapon counts 0.6 × pellets when its first pellet lands on the kart; laser charge is assumed full; direct frisbee hits do no damage to karts; kart bodies are not lag-compensated (riders are).
  - **Proposal:** a Sim event hook (`sim.onEvent(fn)`), or let the combat lane treat Vehicle entities as targets.
- **Bots don't drive.** They also treat karts as unknown dynamic obstacles: the nav grid is static, and the stuck handling copes.
- Terminal kiosks are World-layer colliders created on tick 0. The AI nav grid, built on tick 1, includes them.
- `EntityState.cls` is -1 for vehicles and terminals (see the contract proposal above).
- Karts persist across a match restart. Riders are unseated automatically by the respawn teleport.
- Rapier gotchas found here are logged in `LEARNINGS.jsonl` (7 entries):
  - a downward KCC push while grounded causes dead stops;
  - huge cuboids let shapes sink;
  - query freshness differs for create, move and remove;
  - the grip model;
  - the ground point on slopes;
  - cross-lane fixture gating;
  - the event tap.
- Ideas, out of scope:
  - kart horn "BEEP!" onomatopoeia (L5);
  - driver emotes;
  - a second seat (gunner) — the seat math already supports offsets;
  - the RC plane from the same terminal type.

# U1 handoff: chat, room browser, first-match tips, quality notice

**Status: done and ready to integrate.** Everything is inside `src/client/ui/**`, `server/**` and new tests. Three
small edits to `src/client/main.ts` switch it on (§4). No shared contract changed. `input.ts` needs no change: the
lead's `InputState.suspended` (commit `9af573a`) is used as is.

## 1. What players get

### Chat
- **Enter** or **T** opens the chat input (right column, under the kill feed, above the ammo panel).
  - **Enter** sends. An empty line just closes. **Esc** cancels and discards.
  - It does not open while a menu is up, while another text field has focus, with Ctrl/Alt/Meta held, or on key
    repeat. Gamepads have no chat.
- **Game input is blocked while typing**, in two layers:
  - The input stops keydown propagation, so InputState, the kit picker (E / 1–6) and the scoreboard (Tab) never
    see the keys.
  - `chatOpenChanged` sets `input.suspended`, so held keys and buttons are released too.
  - Keyup is let through on purpose, so a key held when the chat opened still releases.
  - Measured: holding W for 6 s with the chat open moved the corgi **0.00 m**. After closing it, the same hold moved
    it **4.67 m**.
- **Pointer lock** is kept while typing, and the click-to-play overlay is hidden while the chat is open.
  - Chrome drops the lock on Esc and may swallow that keydown. A lock loss while the chat is open therefore closes the
    chat but **keeps the draft** for the next open.
  - A stray click that blurs the field puts focus back in it.
- **Feed**:
  - Sender names are in their team color (from the roster). A line that arrives before the roster names its sender
    is recolored once the roster lands.
  - Lines fade after 8 s (the last second is a fade). At most 6 recent lines show while closed.
  - While the chat is open, the whole history shows (50 lines), scrollable.
  - Server notices appear in the same feed as gold italic system lines.
- **Presence**: the server now announces "Rex joined the yard" and "Rex left the yard" to the rest of the room.
  - These go to the feed only, with no toast.
  - Other server notices (e.g. "server restarting") go to the feed and are still toasted.
- **Safety and truth**:
  - Text is cleaned with the authority's own `cleanText`, max 120 characters.
  - Rendering is `textContent` only. A test fails if `innerHTML` is ever touched.
  - Your line shows **pending** until the server echoes it, then turns normal.
  - With no echo within 4 s (dropped or disconnected), or if the transport refuses it, the line shows **"not
    delivered"**, struck through.
  - The server drops a second line within 0.5 s. The client refuses to send within 0.75 s: the draft stays and the
    hint reads "Easy, pup: one message a second". So nothing silently vanishes.

### Room browser
- The menu's DEPLOY card has **ROOMS ▸** next to SETTINGS. It opens **Online rooms** in the right card:
  - Rooms from the server in the field are listed: name, mode, humans/max + bots, and status (WARMUP / IN PLAY /
    POST-MATCH).
  - Each room has a **JOIN** button, or **FULL** (disabled).
  - The list refreshes every 5 s while it is open.
- **Create**: a room-name field prefilled with a suggestion (e.g. `porch-scuffle-96`), an **Unlisted** toggle and a
  **CREATE ▸** button.
  - Typing an existing name turns the button into JOIN ▸.
  - Unlisted rooms get a `_` prefix. They are joinable by name but never listed (see §3).
- **States**:
  - loading: skeleton rows;
  - ready;
  - empty: "No rooms yet. Start one below and share its name.";
  - error: "Can't reach that server. Is it running?" with a **RETRY** button;
  - busy: 429/503;
  - offline: `navigator.onLine`;
  - bad server address.
  - A failed refresh keeps the last good list on screen, dimmed.
  - Failures back off 5 → 10 → 20 → 30 s.
- **Keyboard and gamepad**: every control is `data-nav`. Focus stays on the same room's JOIN button across
  refreshes. Esc or pad B goes back to the classes view.
- **Joining** uses the normal PLAY flow with `PlayOptions.room` set. The page reloads to
  `?server=…&name=…&cls=…&team=…&room=…`. Checked end to end: menu → ROOMS → JOIN `porch-party` → the server reports
  `porch-party` with 2 humans.
- On a page served by the production server, the field is prefilled with the page's own server, and joins use it.

### First-match tips (FTUE)
There are three tips. Each shows once, and only in your first match:

| Tip | When | Text |
|---|---|---|
| basics | 1.5 s of play after your first spawn | `[WASD] move · [MOUSE] aim · [LMB] fire · hold [RMB] to aim down` |
| kiosk | the first time your team's Ordnance Kiosk is in reach (same rule as its `E change kit` prompt: `findInteractTarget`) | `Press [E] at the Ordnance Kiosk to change kit` |
| moves | after 60 s of play | `Crouch [C] while sprinting [SHIFT] to slide · crouch in the air to ground-pound` |

- **Placement and timing**:
  - The tip is a small paper card at the bottom centre, under the character. It is never over the crosshair, never
    modal and never takes input.
  - Tips only run while you are actually playing: not in menus, the chat or the death screen, and not while the
    pointer is unlocked. Their clocks pause meanwhile.
- **When a tip ends**:
  - after its duration: 10 / 7 / 9 s;
  - early, once you have done the thing, but never before 3 s. "Done" means moved + fired + aimed for basics, and
    slid + ground-pounded for moves;
  - for the kiosk tip, 1.5 s after you walk away.
- **What counts as seen**:
  - A tip counts as seen after 2 s on screen. One cut shorter comes back.
  - "moves" is skipped silently if you already slide and pound.
  - Once the first match you played at least 10 s of ends, tips are done for good.
- **Storage**: `localStorage['cvc.tips']` = `{v:1, seen:[…], done}`. Every access is in try/catch, so throwing or
  garbage storage is fine.
- **Settings › FIRST-MATCH TIPS › SHOW AGAIN** resets it.

### Quality-change notice
What applies live today (read from the code):

| Part | When it applies | Where |
|---|---|---|
| FX particle density | live | `fx.setQuality` |
| audio panning (HRTF ↔ equal-power) | live | `audio.setQuality` |

- When the chosen tier differs from the tier the world was built with, the settings panel (menu and pause) shows:
  "⟳ Grass, shadows and sky change after a reload. Effects and sound switched already." plus a **RELOAD NOW**
  button. Mid-match it adds "Reloading leaves this match."

- Reload drops `?quality=`, which would otherwise override the new choice again.
- Once P2 makes world quality live, pass `createHud(ui, actions, { qualityLive: true })` and the notice never shows.

## 3. Server API (`GET /rooms`)
```
GET http://<server>/rooms   (root of the HTTP port, whatever WS_PATH is)
200 [{ "name": "porch-party", "mode": "team-deathmatch", "players": 4, "humans": 2, "bots": 2, "maxPlayers": 12, "phase": "live" }]
```
- **What is listed**:
  - live public rooms: at least one human, and not unlisted;
  - busiest first, then by name;
  - at most `roomsListMax` rows (default 50). Names are ≤ 24 chars, so the response is bounded (~6 KB).
- **Row fields**: `players` = humans + bots. `maxPlayers` = human slots (`MAX_PLAYERS_PER_ROOM`).
- **Privacy**:
  - The response carries room names and head counts only. There are no IPs, connection ids or player names; a test
    asserts none leak.
  - Rooms named `_…` are **unlisted**. They are joinable by name but never listed. Public `/stats` shows their name
    as `(unlisted)`. Operators (direct loopback, or `&token=STATS_TOKEN`) still see the real name.
  - This covers QA W1's point that room names act as private-room keys.
- **Rate limit**:
  - `/rooms` and `/stats` share one per-IP token bucket (`httpRate` 5/s, `httpBurst` 20). Over it: `429` plus
    `Retry-After`.
  - `/health` is exempt (load balancers).
  - `/stats` had no limit before, so this also closes that gap. The soak/QA tools call `/stats` a few times, far
    under the burst.
- **CORS**: `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *`, or the matching origin when `ALLOWED_ORIGINS` is set. The dev client
  on :5173 reads a server on :8787.
- **Methods**: only GET and HEAD (405 otherwise). `LIST_ROOMS=0` gives 404.
- **Presence**: on a successful join, everyone else in the room gets `{t:'notice', text:'<name> joined the yard'}`.
  On a socket close, the remaining players get `'<name> left the yard'`. Nothing is sent on a room crash or shutdown
  (those kick everyone).

### Proposed, not needed now (shared contracts)
- `ServerMsg` `chat` could carry `team` (and `pid`), e.g. `{ t: 'chat'; from: string; team: TeamId; text: string }`,
  with `src/host/room.ts:210` becoming `this.broadcast({ t: 'chat', from: p.name, team: p.team, text })`. Today the

  `hud.chat(from, text)` would gain a `team?` parameter.
- `host/guard.ts` `cleanText` strips `\n` and `\t` outright instead of turning them into spaces (`"a\nb"` becomes

## 6. Known issues and follow-ups
- **Chat channel**: "ALL" only. There is no team chat, mute, report or word filter (not in scope).
- **Team colors** come from a roster lookup by name. This is ambiguous with duplicate names; see the proposal in §4.
- **Presence detection is by text**: `PRESENCE_RE` matches the exact server strings "… joined the yard" / "… left
  the yard". If those strings change in `server/app.ts`, update `chat-model.ts`. A `kind` field on `notice` would be
  sturdier (shared contract).
- **Presence is online only.** The offline worker room never has joiners.
- **Tips show keyboard keys**, including to gamepad players.
- **The moves tip needs 60 s of active play.** It pauses in menus, chat, death and the unlocked state, so a player
  who dies a lot sees it later.
- **Unlisted is not private.** Anyone who guesses `_name` can join; there are no passwords.
- **The room list assumes one mode per server**: every room uses the server's `MODE`, so the Mode column is the
  same for all rows today.
- **Dead-server console noise**: with the room browser open on a dead server, the browser logs one
  `ERR_CONNECTION_REFUSED` per attempt (backed off to 30 s). This matters only if an e2e opens the rooms view and
  asserts 0 console errors.
- **HTTP limit on `/stats`**: `/stats` is now rate-limited as well (5/s, burst 20 per IP). A monitor that polls
  faster from one address needs `HTTP_RATE`.
- **Unused engine quality profiles**: `engine/quality.ts` `QUALITY` profiles are unused; that is P2's lane. The
  notice text names only what really changes on reload.
- **Lead-owned docs**: `PROGRESS.md` and `ARCHITECTURE.md` are not edited. Suggested lines are in §4.

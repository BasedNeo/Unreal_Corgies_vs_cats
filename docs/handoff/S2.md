# S2 handoff: vehicle engines, vehicle and destructible voices, adventure stingers

**Status: done, ready to integrate.**
- **No `main.ts` change is needed.** `audio.update(ctx.camera, states, localId, dt)` and `audio.onGameEvent(ev)` already
  receive everything.
- **No shared contract changed.** Everything is inside `src/client/audio/**` and `tests/unit/audio*`.
- **Two small extras beyond the listed events** are flagged in §6: a kart horn voice, and no footsteps while seated.

## 1. What plays when

### Engine loops (driven from `update(states)`, ≤ 30 Hz)
| Vehicle | When it runs | Sound | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mower kart** (`Vehicle`, `cls` ≤ 0) | It has a rider (`weapon` ≥ 0) and isn't `Dead`. | A two-stroke "putt-putt":<br>• discrete putts at **10.5 Hz** idle, rising to **36 Hz** at 15 m/s;<br>• boost (`Sprinting`) ×1.22, drift (`Crouching`) ×1.1, airborne (not `Grounded`) ×1.18;<br>• brighter and louder with speed;<br>• a lumpy wobble at idle. | **Your own kart** is centered (2D) at `localGain` 0.45.<br>**Others** are spatial at `remoteGain` 0.4, ref 3.5 m, culled beyond 40 m. |
| **RC plane** (`Vehicle`, `cls` = 1) | It has a pilot. | A buzzy toy prop:<br>• **92 → 250 Hz** with throttle (from `unpackPlaneAux`), +2.2 Hz per m/s of airspeed above 8;<br>• blade chop at 0.2 × pitch;<br>• low-pass opens with throttle and airspeed;<br>• rushing air ∝ airspeed², ×0.4 on wheels;<br>• **boost**: a roar layer (brown noise + sub square an octave down), pitch ×1.12;<br>• **stall**: a 6.3 Hz cough on the gain, pitch ×0.8;<br>• level follows throttle. | **Yours** is centered.<br>**Others** are spatial, ref 8 m, culled beyond 70 m. Remote loops bend pitch with radial speed (a toy doppler, clamped to ×0.82…×1.22). |
| Rider leaves, vehicle removed, or it goes out of range | — | The pitch spools down and the level fades (τ 0.12 s). Sources stop on the audio clock 0.7 s later, and the graph is disconnected on the next update. | — |

**Selection rules:**
- At most **4 audible loops**, nearest first. The local rider's own vehicle always gets one.
- Hysteresis stops slot churn: a playing loop ranks as if it were 20 % nearer, and it is kept to 1.15 × the cull
  distance.

**Budget:** each loop holds one `engine` voice in the shared `VoiceLimiter`, at priority 1 for your own and 0 for others.
- Loops acquire with `canSteal = false`, so **a loop never cuts a one-shot off**.
- A one-shot *can* steal a loop. Footsteps are priority 0 and newer, so in a busy fight remote engines go first. The loop
  notices on its next update, fades, and retries after 0.5 s.

**Mix:**
- Loops play through an **engines sub-bus** that **ducks −40 %** at full combat intensity.
- Voices are low-passed below the footstep and shot bands.
- The kart is high-passed at 70 Hz: its 10–36 Hz firing fundamental would otherwise pump the master compressor.

**Local vehicle events** play centered at 0.8× gain and one priority higher. An event counts as local when the vehicle's
rider is the local pet, or when it happens within 3 m of the local pet (interpolated states lag about 0.1 s).

**Chapter finish:** the last step emits `step`, `chapter` and `win` on one tick. The step jingle waits a frame and is
dropped when the fanfare comes, and the fanfare mutes the team sting for 2.4 s. Result: the fanfare plays alone.

**Unchanged:**
- Class abilities and E1's sniper cues keep their voices (`dot_paint` → `dotPing`, …).
- Anything else unmapped still gets `whoomp`.
- Every other `score` reason still plays the team sting.

## 3. Levels: "listening by numbers"
**Method:**
- Headless Chromium 1194, `OfflineAudioContext` at 48 kHz.
- Each recipe goes through gain → destination with **no master compressor or limiter**.
- **K** is a K-weighted level (BS.1770-style: a +4 dB shelf at 1.5 kHz, a 38 Hz high-pass; −0.691 + 10 log₁₀ mean square).
  **K100** is its maximum over 100 ms windows.
- **FS band** is power in 2–3.5 kHz, the footstep band. Units are consistent across every row.
- The loops are the real `VehicleLoops`, driven by `ctx.suspend()` every 13 render quanta (34.7 ms, one manager update
  each). The listener stands at the origin, 1.5 m up.

**Mix verdict:**
- **Engines under combat:**
  - Your kart at top speed sits 6.6 dB (K) under a local Squeaker shot and 15 dB under a snap. Your plane at cruise
    sits 5 dB under a Squeaker shot; a brief boost sits 1 dB under.
  - Remote engines sit 10–20 dB lower still.
  - The sub-bus ducks a further 4.4 dB at full combat intensity.
  - Shots' footstep-band energy (−11.6) sits ≥ 24 dB over any engine.
- **Footsteps are never masked:**
  - A footstep 5 m away measures about −32.3 in the band (from its 0.8 gain and the panner falloff).
  - Every engine sits under that: local kart ≤ −47.0, local plane cruise −39.1, remote plane overhead −42.1, remote
    kart −62.0.
  - The tightest case is the local plane boosting (−36.4): 4 dB margin.
- **Clean starts and stops:**
  - The first 5 ms of every loop peak at 0.0000 (it fades in from 0.0001).
  - Every loop ends in exact silence after its stop.
  - The limiter was never needed in the renders: 0 stolen, 0 rejected.

## 6. Known issues and notes
**Beyond the listed events (flagged for the lead):**
- **The horn.** V1's `ability 'horn'` also fell through to `whoomp`, so it gets `honk`. To drop it, delete the `horn:`
  line in `VEHICLE_SFX`.
- **No footsteps while seated.** A seated rider is `Mounted | Grounded` and moves at the vehicle's velocity, so the
  footstep system played a running patter for every driver. The fix is one condition in `stepCb`.

**Vehicles:**
- **A plane's `jump` (lift-off) still plays the character `boing`, and its `land` (touchdown) the `thud`.** The existing
  jump/land cases don't know about vehicles. This was not on the card; it is a one-line vehicle check if wanted.
- The engine keys on a rider. An empty kart rolling downhill, or a pilotless plane gliding after a bail, is silent.

**Mix:**
- The tightest footstep margin is the local plane boosting near the ground: 4 dB (§3).
- **Existing L5 recipes peak over 1.0** before the master limiter at their play gains: `snap` 1.17, `thwack` 1.10, `boom`
  1.10, `whoomp` 1.04. The limiter catches them. They are unchanged because they are not on this card.

**Engine code:**
- `setQuality('low')` changes the panning model only for loops started afterwards, the same as for one-shots.
- `weather.ts` (G1) still calls `Math.hypot` once per sprinkler per frame, which allocates. Left as is: it is G1's file.

**Why no sack parapet along the trench lip:** B2's kart grid treats any prop top more than 0.26 m over the ground within
0.55 m as a ledge, so a row of sacks on a sloping bank is a kart wall; the first version walled both kart lines off
(`driveLineClear` false). An earthen spoil berm (terrain) was tried too: tall enough to hide a chest from the floor it
made > 0.6 m cell steps (the kart limit). Salient positions keep both: cover where you fight, open ground where you drive.

**Lanes / gates (design intent).** Each FOB is a *front line* south (corgis) of the ch3 getaway lane and a *compound*
behind it (kiosks, flag, generator, armory, motor pool, tower). Three gates per FOB, all ≥ 6 m wide and tested open for
bots: the west gate + main path (x −60..−38), the kart gate (x −24..−12, between a hedgehog and the east wall; the
Kart-O-Matic's exit corridor is kept 3.2 m clear either side for 22 m) and the east lane under the watchtower. Cover
rhythm along each lane: FOB wall → hedgehog / X-barricade (≈ 8 m out) → crater / low cover (≈ 15–20 m) → trench line
(≈ 38 m out) → the centre pads. Defenders shoot over two-sack walls (0.76 m: chest hidden, head shows, the 1.25 m
aim pivot clears them — tested with the real hitscan rays); the watchtower overlooks the approach, not the enemy spawn
(110 m away); no nest or tower sees a spawn point.

**Battle marks (visual, `BattleLayout`)**: mud (a 13 m churned compound scar per base + plaza/gates/motor pool/behind
the walls), scorch (gates, walls, craters), standing puddles (crater floors, trench floors, trodden hollows), tyre
ruts (both kart exits + the ch3 getaway line), paw-print trails (spawns → kiosks → gates → trenches), spent casings
behind every firing position, spent tennis balls, splinters; damaged fence sections behind both bases and on the
yard corners (snapped/askew/scorched boards, bullet holes, plywood patches; colliders unchanged).

**Bookmarks (new)**: `corgi_fob`, `cat_fob`, `corgi_spawn`, `cat_spawn`, `midfield`, `corgi_tower`.

## 4. Iterations that mattered (one change per loop, before/after at the same cameras)
1. **Layout vs the sim** — the first corgi FOB had its centre wall and armory on the straight ch3 getaway line and
   inside V1's random-drive start: the A2 escape test stalled 29 s against the crates, the random drive wedged at 90 m
   (needs 150). → a *front line* south of the getaway lane and a *compound* north of it; the lane is now a test.
2. **Craters** — the `bowl` op is absolute (it made a 1.4 m pit on the rise and a bump in the dip) → `hole` + a ring
   of small `raise` rims (0.5–0.6 m everywhere).
3. **Sacks read as bricks, budget** — beveled 92-tri sacks → a 44-tri single-chamfer box (`seg: 1`, prim-mesh) on the
   top row and cheap smooth blocks below: they now read as pillowy sacks at 5 m (`iter2/cats.png`) for half the cost.
4. **FOBs did not read** (`iter1/corgi_fob.png`: low junk, a net seen edge-on as a dark frame) → lean-to camo nets with
   a burlap fringe, bigger torn banners (TSL icons), pennants on the towers, garden-sign armor on the nests, lighter
   olive crates with pale stencils, a trampled-earth compound scar (`iter3/*`).
5. **Walls on slopes** — walls sat on the lowest ground under them, so on a slope a "chest-high" wall was 0.6 m on its
   high side (the cover test caught it) → walls add the mean-minus-min ground (`lift`) to their height.

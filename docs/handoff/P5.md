## 2. Item 1: the exposure follows the time of day

**What changed.**
- **The exposure.** `STYLE_EXPOSURE` (in `style-webgpu.js`) is a shared uniform, multiplied into the linear HDR colour
  just before tone mapping. This is the same maths as `renderer.toneMappingExposure`, at the cost of one multiply and
  no pass.
  - The sky writes it from a new time-of-day ramp, `YARD_RAMPS.exposure`, keyed by sun elevation.
  - It is 1 while the sun is low (dusk, t = 0.74, e = 2.6°) and at night.
  - It falls to **0.78 from 16° up**: 1.25 × 0.78 = 0.975, the pre-P4 exposure less the ~2 % that P4's lighter
    vignette adds.
  - **A storm keeps the lift at any hour** (`lerp(ramp, 1, storm)`).
- **The fill.** P4's dusk sky fill (`rimI`) now returns to its base level by **18°** (it was ×1.6 at 16° and ×1.1 at
  30°). The dusk keys (e ≤ 6°) are P4's.

**Why both.** An exposure-only sweep (`sweeps/exp1`) left match start high: at ×0.8, overview and center were
**+6.2 / +6.9 %** over pre-P4, because the fill was still ×1.53 at 18°. With the fill back to ×1 by 18° they were
**+2.8 / +1.7 %**.

## 3. Item 2: the sky fill stays out of interiors

**The material** (`style-webgpu.js`):
- **The open factor.** Every hardened material computes `hardOpen` once per fragment, before lighting: 1 in the open,
  0 inside an interior. It is a loop over `STYLE_INTERIORS`, a fixed `uniformArray` of 16 boxes (two vec4 each)
  with a live count.
- **What it masks.** `HardenedLightingModel.direct` multiplies **only the fill light's colour** by it. The fill is
  the sky's anti-sun directional, flagged `userData.skyFill` in `createStyleLights` and in the sky. So the fill's
  diffuse and specular both stay out.
- **Characters.** P4's rim + fill keeps `STYLE.interior.charKeep` (25 %) inside.
- **The test point.** It is the air in front of the surface: the point `probe` 0.45 m along the normal, feathered
  over 0.4 m from the box faces.
  - An inner wall faces into the box, so it is shut.
  - The outer shell and the roof face out of it, so they stay lit.
  - Light spills 0.4 m into a mouth.
- **Cost.** No new light, pass, draw or program variant.
  - The West Yard has no interiors, so the loop runs zero times and its pixels are unchanged.
  - On The Lot it is 10 box tests, about 12 ALU each, per fragment.
- **CPU twin.** `interiorOpenAt()` is the same test in JavaScript, used by the tests.

**The data** (`the-lot.ts` `lotInteriors`, from the same placements as the geometry; seed-stable, 10 boxes):
- **8 pipe bores**, one per segment. Each runs mouth to mouth, with the inner facets' apothem across it. The firing
  windows between tunnel segments stay lit.
- **2 site-office containers.** Each box spans the closed walls, from 0.5 m under the floor slab to the roof's top.
  - Only the open ends lie on box faces.
  - My first box sat on the inner faces, and left a lit lilac seam where the walls meet the floor and the ceiling:
    the feather reached them (`sweeps/int1`, looked at). The test pins the seams now.
- The sky hands the boxes to the style: `createYardSky({ interiors })` calls `setStyleInteriors`, and `dispose`
  clears only its own list.

## 4. Item 3: The Lot's storm overview

**Diagnosis.** The storm overview is distance fog, not light:
- the camera is at 114 m, and the look point is ~215 m away;
- the storm's fog multiplier (×3.0) made the centre ~62 % fog and the far side ~85 %;
- `sky.update`'s height factor was already floored at 0.5 for cameras above 77 m.

**The fix.** That floor now falls with the weather's fog multiplier: `floor / wxFog ^ STYLE.mood.highFog.wx`, with
wx 0.5, which gives 0.29 in storm. It is a token, so the sweep could set it from the page.
- **Unchanged:** cameras under ~77 m (players, the lineups, the West Yard overview at 72 m), and clear weather at any
  height (unit test).

**Sweep** (`sweeps/fog1`, luma / contrast; the Lot overview, t = 0.74):

| wx | storm | default (overcast opening) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 (HEAD) | 51.0 / 18.8 | 63.2 / 34.2 |
| **0.5 (kept)** | **38.4 / 23.5** | **57.1 / 35.0** |
| 0.75 | 33.2 / 24.9 | 54.4 / 35.2 |
| 1.0 | 33.0 / 25.0 | 51.9 / 35.3 |

- I kept 0.5. Most of the gain is there, and the default overview barely moves.
- **Mean luma falls** (51 → 38), because the fog colour was the bright part of the frame. Judge this item by contrast

**What I saw** (`compare/overview-storm.png`, `overview-default.png`):

## 7. Lead snippet (`src/client/world/world-view.ts`, not my file)

One line in the `createYardSky` call hands the world's interiors to the sky:
```ts
  const sky = createYardSky(scene, {
    timeOfDay: opts.timeOfDay ?? data.timeOfDay ?? 0.68,
    shadowMap: opts.shadowMapSize ?? P.shadowMapSize,
    clouds: P.clouds,
    fogScale: 118 / (edge + 1), // W9 L3: the fog's distances scale with the map (West Yard: 118 / 118 = 1)
    interiors: data.interiors, // W10 P5: the sky fill stays out of these boxes (tunnels, containers)
  });
```
- **Without it:** items 1 and 3 work, and the interiors stay lit (count 0).
- **With it:** every number above. All my Lot measurements used it.

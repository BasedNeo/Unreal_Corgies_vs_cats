# E1 handoff — Madame Pointillé, the Siamese sniper elite (Wave 4)

**Status:** done for integration. A second boss for the **laser-pointer duel** of chapter 4 ("Laser Pointer at
Dawn"): authoritative, deterministic, spawned through the same `spawnBoss(sim, at?, { boss })` path as the
Vac-Tank, drawn through the same `createBossAvatar()` / `createBossTelegraphFx()` the lead already wires.
The Vac-Tank is unchanged (its 17 + 6 tests pass). **Lead edits needed** (paste-ready, §5): the boss bar's phase
label, `?boss=<id>` routing, and (recommended) a "DOT ON YOU" cue + four audio cues.

**Design pitch (original IP).** *Madame Pointillé, the Dot Artiste*: an elite Siamese sniper, 1.4× a cat, in a
tilted crimson beret with a black stalk, a black turtleneck, a crimson ascot whose white-striped tails stream over
her left shoulder, and a paintbrush tucked behind her right ear. She is a pretentious art critic who "paints" her
subjects with a laser dot before every shot ("Hold still, darlings. I am painting." — "My LENS!" — "Fine. Abstract
expressionism it is!"). Phase 2: she flings her beret away and her tail puffs into a bottle brush. Nothing
references Conker/Rare or any other IP; all lines are original (`BOSS_BARKS.madame_pointille`).

## 2. Design and numbers

**Where: three perches across the lawn from the garage Rooftops** (the patio side). They sit on visual-only
surfaces; she is **kinematic** (`e.moveFrozen = true` every tick: movement and map effects skip her), so nothing
under her needs a collider, and on defeat the combat lane's corpse physics makes her **tumble off**.

| Perch (`MADAME_POINTILLE.perches`) | Feet | From the roof perches | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| `patio_umbrella` (start) | (23, 12.1, -90) | 58–81 m | mid range, 12 m up; the patio table beneath is cover from her |
| `house_eave` | (-5, 30.1, -97.5) | 86–110 m | long range, 30 m up, sees over most cover (the counter: range) |
| `grill_lid` | (43.4, 5.6, -91.3) | 44–66 m | close and low: the garage parapet hides a roof corgi's chest (she can still see heads) |

Measured (`perches` test): umbrella duels 3/3 roof perches · 151 open ground vantages (12–60 m) that can shoot
her, 60 with full cover ≤ 8 m · house 3/3 · 122 (36) · grill 2/3 · 115 (48). Sightlines are Rapier raycasts both
ways (her lens → a corgi's chest/head; a corgi's 1.25 m aim pivot → her chest/head), like D3.

**The kit (one attack + a flush-out):**

```
idle (0.5–1.0 s) ─pick─▶ TRACK: the dot appears 3.2 m beside the target, sweeps onto its chest (head if the chest is
    behind cover) at 8.5 m/s (p2 11.5); painted time builds only with line of sight, drains ×1.5 while hidden;
    0.45 s out of sight = lock lost (`dot_lost`, re-acquire from zero). Last 0.45 s: the device GLINTS.
    At 1.1 s (p2 0.85 s) of painted tracking she fires along the dot: it hits only the painted target, only if the
    dot is on it (44 dmg, p2 48; knockback 4) → recover 1.3 s (the punish window).
  ─the target hid ≥ 1.2 s─▶ LOB: 0.7 s wind-up, 1 hairball (p2: 2, ±2.8 m) at its last seen spot, warning circle
    for the whole 1.4 s flight (≥ 1.15 s in p2), blast r 3.2 / 34 dmg / knockback 9 (flushes you out). Cooldown 7 s.
  ─every 16–22 s (p2 10–14) · 14 % max hp taken on one perch · an enemy within 5.5 m · phase 2─▶ LEAP:
    0.45 s crouch (the destination is announced: `sniper_leap` + a marker), parabolic leap(s) (≤ 36 m each;
    house↔grill hops via the umbrella), 0.35 s landing. Vulnerable throughout.
50 % hp: PHASE SHIFT 1.6 s (beret flies off, taunt) → forced relocation → phase 2 (faster dot + clock).
Defeat: taunt, `score` (standalone), tumbles off the perch, removed after 5 s.
```

**Fairness.** Every hit has ≥ 1.1 s (p2 0.85 s, floor `MIN_TELEGRAPH` 0.8 s) of painted tracking since its `dot_paint`
(the paint tick itself counts as the first warning frame, not as tracked time). The shot goes exactly along the
beam clients draw (lens + yaw/pitch), so the dot shows where it lands. Cover always works; sprinting across her line
of fire outruns the phase-1 dot (test); phase 2 needs cover or the glint. Invulnerable (fresh-spawn) targets are
never picked. Hairball circles are truthful (shared hairball code).

**Weak point: the glinting laser device.** A sphere r 0.24 m at the lens (aim pivot 0.9 m up, 0.17 m to her right,
1.44 m along the aim — matched to the model's muzzle within 6 mm). A shot through it: **×2, crit**; during the glint
it also **spoils her shot** (`shot_spoiled`, 1.5 s stagger, no shot). Head-zone hits keep the combat lane's own
head multiplier. Hitscan uses the lag-compensated body position.

**HP.** `2200 + 1600 × (squad − 1)`; squad = humans + corgi bots (bots weight 1, like the Vac-Tank). 1 → 2.2k,
4 → 7k.

**Balance** (headless West Yard; a scripted Overwatch marksman = human stand-in, see §6):
- solo marksman, seeds 1–8: **125–242 s** (median ~208 s), marksman deaths 2–7; 7/8 inside 2–4 min.
- marksman + 3 L3 corgi bots (hp 7k): **62–107 s**.
- She only fires when the target stays painted: every shot she fired in the duels hit; hiding always worked.

**Snapshot encoding (no protocol change).** `cls` = 1 (BOSSES index); `yaw/pitch` = the laser aim; **`ammo` = dot
distance along the aim in cm (0 = beam off)**; `weapon` = 2 (laser_longshot); `flags` = EFlag bits + the boss state
in bits 16–26 (`packBossFlags`: `SniperAct`, stage, phase 2, progress = track progress, so the glint is its tail);
`Grounded|Aiming` on a perch, `Firing` at the shot, `anim` Jump/Fall in the air.

**Events** (`ability`, id = the boss): `dot_paint` (x,y,z = first dot) · `dot_glint` (lens) · `dot_lost` · `shot_spoiled`
(lens) · `sniper_leap` (destination feet) · `beret_off` · `boss_intro` · `hairball_windup` / `hairball_mortar` (circle).
The shot is an ordinary `fire` (wpn 2) → L5 draws the laser beam and plays the zap; `land`/`jump` on leaps.

**Model.** 5,486 tris (budget 15k), 5 draw calls (body, rifle, rifle ink, emitter glow, beret), 47 bones, 1.78 m.

**Cost.** Boss systems with the sniper, 4 bots, 60 s: p50 0.02 ms, p95 0.09 ms per tick (load ~15 on 4 cores).

```ts

  trigger: { type: 'reach', params: { x: 69.1, z: -49.1, radius: 3 } } },   // D3's roof_parapet; see note
{ id: 'duel', text: 'Out-snipe Madame Pointillé', trigger: { type: 'defeat', params: { boss: 'madame_pointille' } },
```

- **Spawn** when the `defeat` step with `params.boss` starts: `if (!findBoss(sim, id)) spawnBoss(sim, undefined, { boss: id })`
  — she drops onto the patio umbrella (a 1.8 s invulnerable intro). Complete the step when that entity is `dead`
  (or gone). She scales with the squad at spawn (1 human + 3 bots → 7k hp, ~1–2 min with bots helping).
- Chapter `t: 0.27` (dawn) reads well (`artifacts/e1-duel-house-dawn.png`); start the squad near the garage
  (e.g. `start: { x: 60, z: -40 }`), 40–90 m from her perches.

  x 68–94, z -70..-48). If the trigger can check feet height, require `y ≥ 6.5`.
- `botToPlayerDamage` has no `adventure` entry, so her shot is a full 44 against humans there (skirmish/boss-rush
  scale it ×0.42 like any bot/boss).

## 7. Known issues / next

- **Human certification needed** for the duel length and the threat level (stand-in marksman only).
- Perches are West Yard coordinates in data. If the patio, umbrella, grill or house eave move, update
  `WEST_YARD_SNIPER_PERCHES`; the perch/route tests catch lost sightlines and blocked arcs, not visual changes.
- The corgi spawns are 20–90 m from her perches: in boss-rush/skirmish she paints players at the corgi base (fresh
  spawns are skipped while spawn-protected).
- Nobody can reach her perches (she is untouchable in melee); Warden/Breacher players need range or splash.
- `ammo` carries her dot distance: any UI that shows a *remote* entity's ammo would show a large number for her
  (none does today). The dot distance is not interpolated between snapshots (30 Hz): it can step slightly.
- The "painted" ring is client-side cosmetic (nearest character to the dot).
- Bots (L3) do not dodge the dot: in squad fights bots take most of her shots.
- Sim workaround kept from B1: `e.cls = CLASS_IDS[bossIndex]` (her `cls` is 'infiltrator'; she never presses buttons).
- Lab screenshots on the full West Yard take ~50 s each headless (SwiftShader).

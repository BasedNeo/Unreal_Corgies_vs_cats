# L2 handoff — West Yard world (Wave 1)

Status: **done for integration.** The West Yard hub at pet scale (200 × 200 m play area, fence 8 m, characters
1.2 m): deterministic terrain, ~1350 visual prims + 210 colliders from one prop catalog, trampoline jump pads,
wading water, out-of-bounds guard, and a styled client view (grid-exact toon terrain, merged inked props, instanced
board fences, near-field foliage with wind, stylized sky + time of day + fog + camera-following sun shadow).
All `createWorldData` / `buildStaticWorld` / `buildSimWorld` / `worldSystems` / `createWorldView` signatures are
unchanged (extra params are optional). No shared contract outside my paths was touched.

## The map (pet scale; corgi jump 1.47 m / double 2.55 m, cat 1.84 / 3.1, autostep 0.45)
- **North / Corgi base:** 28 m house wall (siding, windows, sliding door) · raised deck 3 m (36 × 16 m) with 10-step stairs
  (ramp collider, 23°) · blue doghouse (enterable, walkable roof via the kibble sack) · parkour: brick steps → AC unit
  (3.4 m) → running jump onto the deck · patio with kettle grill, umbrella table, pots · corgi mini-trampoline in front of the deck.
- **South-east / Cats base:** red board-and-batten shed (walkable 24° roof, ridge 10.6 m) · 5-level cat-tree tower (1.2 m
  steps) · plank bridge from the tower top onto the shed roof · cardboard boxes · cats mini-trampoline.
- **Center:** trampoline (pad top 1.25 m, launch 17.5 m/s ≈ 7.6 m peak) · two lawn chairs (walk under the seat) · picnic
  table (3 m top, walk under) · hedge islands (3.3 m: full cover) · wheelbarrow · beach ball · hose + sprinkler.
- **West flank:** 3 raised garden beds (1.3 m) · big tree (roots = walkable ramps to 3 m, branch stub 3.9 m, limb perch 5 m;
  canopy 19–40 m) · sandbox (sunken sand, castle) · back mound (4.3 m) + birdbath · dug holes under the fence.
- **East flank:** swing set + slide (run up the chute to a 5 m deck) · pond (wading, 1.5 m deep) with rocks, cattails, lily
  pads, frog statue · gnome · kiddie pool (1.1 m rim, wading) with a rubber duck · tall hedge corridor along the fence.
- **Neighbour strips** (x ∈ ±[100, 114], z ∈ [-70, 70]): flank corridors entered through fence gaps at z = ±45
  (west: dug holes under raised boards; east: broken boards 1.55 m / open gap). Compost bin, tire, firewood, ladder, buckets.
- 12 spawns (6 per team) on dry flat lawn in front of each base, ≥ 5 m from props, facing the map; opposite teams > 100 m apart.
- Invisible 'boundary' blockers above every fence; out-of-bounds guard returns characters to a spawn.

Honest critique (harsh art-director pass, after 6 iterations):
- **Works:** the pet-scale read is strong at ground level — the 28 m house and 3 m deck tower over 1.2 m characters,
  the neighbour-strip corridor between 8 m fences is the best shot. Bases read instantly by color (blue doghouse/
  shutters/banner vs red shed/cat tree). Golden hour sells the style (long fence and tree shadows across mowing
  stripes). Night is moonlit blue, never black.
- **Weak:** the overview's far haze is flat and beige-green, and the ground beyond the fences is an endless mowed field
  (neighbour yards have houses/trees but no fences or props). The sun halo washes the sky in the deck view. Clover
  near the camera reads as flat stickers; the trampoline mat is too dark to read as bouncy. The cat-tree tower is small
  seen from mid-field and the plank bridge reads as a thin pole. Canopy blobs share one displacement per radius, so
  some blobs look alike. The beach ball is six overlapping squashed spheres (possible z-fight at grazing angles).
- Iterations that mattered: fixed ink-hull copies on foliage (see LEARNINGS), removed lawn camouflage blotches
  (shading-normal flattening + calmer patches + mowing stripes), re-oriented the house roof, sized daisies/clover to
  pet scale, made the tree organic (no crease ink on bark), moved the overview camera, added altitude-aware fog.

## Integration notes for the lead
1. **Time of day:** `worldView.setTimeOfDay(t)` (0 midnight, .25 sunrise, .5 noon, .75 sunset; default 0.68 from
   `WorldData.timeOfDay`). Suggested in `main.ts`: `if (params.has('t')) worldView.setTimeOfDay(Number(params.get('t')))`;
   for a running cycle pass `createWorldView(scene, data, { daySpeed: 1 / 1200 })` (20-minute day).
2. **Quality:** `createWorldView(scene, data, { quality: 'low' | 'med' | 'high' })`. Headless SwiftShader is ~0.3–1 fps
   on this loaded box with the full world (post pipeline dominates even with the world hidden, ~1.5 fps); if the e2e
   smoke's "60 frames in 60 s" gets tight, pass `quality: 'low'` for `?webgl` runs.
3. **Lights/fog ownership:** the world view drives `createStyleLights()`'s rig as the sun (key light gets `castShadow`, a
   2048² map following the camera, texel-snapped), sky rim and hemisphere by time of day, and sets `scene.fog`
   (FogExp2) and `scene.background`. Don't re-set them elsewhere.
4. **Client prediction (L4):** call `stepWorldEffects(worldData, e, TICK_DT)` right after `stepCharacter()` for the
   predicted player, else trampolines/water mispredict. It mutates only `e.vel`/`e.char` and returns `{ bounced, wading, outOfBounds }`.
5. **Terrain collider is a trimesh now** (card said heightfield; evidence above). L3's `src/sim/ai/nav.ts:97` detects the
   terrain by `ShapeType.HeightField`; switch it to `isTerrainCollider(c)` from `src/sim/world/build.ts` (nav still builds
   in ~108 ms and all 33 L3 tests pass either way). Heightfield remains available via `TERRAIN_COLLIDER`.
6. **Spawn facing:** spawns face into the map, but the in-game shot faces the house: `main.ts` copies `s.yaw` into
   `input.yaw` on `'connected'`, before the first snapshot exists. Copy it on the first state for `localEntity` instead.
7. **Debug bookmarks:** `worldView.bookmarks` (`{ name, pos, look, fov }`) → fill `debug.bookmarks` like `labs/world.ts` does.
8. **Style system:** move `WORLD_EXTRA` into `PALETTE` and `toonNoInk` into `style-webgpu.js` when convenient. Anyone
   opting a toon material out of ink must clear BOTH `isMeshToonNodeMaterial` and `isMeshToonMaterial`.
   The sky dome is an unlit `MeshBasicNodeMaterial` (never inked, glow-like by design).
9. **FX (L5):** pad bounces emit the existing `{ e: 'jump', double: false }`; FX can test the entity position against
   `worldData.jumpPads` for a bounce ring. No new GameEvent types.
10. **Nav/AI:** `surfaceAt`, `occupiedAt`, `nearestPropDist`, `waterAt`, `jumpPadAt` in `src/shared/world/queries.ts` are
    exact, allocation-light and handle pitched boxes; prefer them to vertical Rapier rays for ground queries.
11. Suggested `ARCHITECTURE.md` lines: `world/west-yard.ts` map data · `world/terrain.ts` grid-exact terrain ·
    `world/queries.ts` spatial queries · `sim/world/build.ts` trimesh + props · `client/world/*` view modules.

## Known issues / next

  or cache (all inputs are pure data).
- Foliage is near-field only (58 m); overviews show no grass by design. Tufts/daisies use up-normals (lit like the lawn).
- Visual-only details without colliders: hose line, bed plants, lily pads, stepping stones, rubber duck, picnic-table
  braces; simplified colliders: chair/table legs as boxes, hose reel box, pool wall as 18 segments.
- Doghouse roof access (sack + double jump) and the swing-set slide climb are designed but not covered by tests.
- No `Anim.Swim` when wading (movement lane); water only damps horizontal velocity and caps falls at 6 m/s.
- Weather (MASTER_PLAN §7.10 state machine) not started — sky ramps/fog/cloud uniforms are ready to be driven.
- No new npm dependencies were needed.

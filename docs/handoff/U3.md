# U3 handoff: match awards, the kill feed's throwable glyph, Base Assault tips (Wave 10, ux-3)

**Status: done for integration. Nothing is committed or staged.** Three pieces, all client-side presentation, no
protocol, sim or shared change:

1. **Match awards.** 3 to 5 comic awards on a card just after a PvP match ends, chosen from what happened. The local
   player's own award is highlighted.
2. **The kill feed names the killing weapon.** It reads `death.wpn` (C10): the squeaker grenade or the hairball bomb
   in its faction tape, the gun otherwise, and exactly the old guesses when `wpn` is absent.
3. **Base Assault first-match tips.** Four tips through the U1 tip scheduler, during the player's first Base Assault
   match: steal / carry / capture, return, "our ball must be home", escort.

The new code is inert until the lead pastes the wiring in §5 (`hud.ts` and `main.ts`; both files are outside my paths
and untouched in the working tree).

## 1. The awards, and why

### What the card gives (game-design-psychology: recognition drives the next queue)
The awards celebrate **varied play**, not only knockouts. Each card spreads them across players before anyone gets a
second one. The names are original and work for both species (a cat can be a GUARD DOG, which is the joke).

| Award | Earned by (the least that earns it) | Modes | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **BEST IN SHOW** | top roster score (≥ 1); a tie is broken like the scoreboard (knockouts, then fewest knockouts taken) | all four | roster |
| **SPECIAL DELIVERY** | most captures (≥ 1) | Base Assault | events |
| **GUARD DOG** | carriers knocked out + balls returned (≥ 2) | Base Assault | events + roster |
| **STICKY PAWS** | most steals from the stand (≥ 2); a pick-up of a dropped ball is not a steal | Base Assault | events |
| **MARATHON** | longest single carry, in seconds (≥ 12) | Base Assault | events |
| **PAD PATROL** | most Core Pads taken (≥ 2) | Core Rush | roster |
| **HEAVY PAWS** | most knockouts (≥ 3) | all four | roster |
| **ON A ROLL** | most knockouts in one life (≥ 3) | all four | events |
| **LOB STAR** | most throwable knockouts (≥ 1: the blast never one-shots, so every one took setup). The medal and the line follow the throwable ("2 hairball knockouts") | all four | events (C10 `wpn`) |
| **UNDERDOG** | most knockouts while your team trailed (≥ 3) | PvP only (not Skirmish) | events + MatchState |
| **BULLSEYE** | most critical hits (≥ 5) | all four | events |
| **CHEW TOY** | most damage soaked (≥ 400): the front line's award | all four | events |
| **FIRST BITE** | the match's first knockout | all four | events |

### How the card chooses (`pickAwards`, pure and deterministic)
- **Empty categories** (below the minimum) are skipped.
- **Ties at the top are shared** by up to 3 winners. The names are joined ("Rex & Cat 6") and a TIE tag is shown. A
  wider tie is no achievement, so the award is dropped.
- **Order of picking:**
  1. BEST IN SHOW always goes first.
  2. Then the mode's own awards, then the fights. An award is taken only if it has a winner who has no award yet, up
     to 5.
  3. If there are fewer than 3, the card fills with second awards.
  4. **The local player's own:** if they earned a category the card left out, it goes on. On a full card it replaces
     the last pick, never BEST IN SHOW. Each client's card is its own.
- **Nothing earned → no card** (the empty state is no panel, and the scoreboard opens as before).

### Sources: the client's own stream, never sim state
- **Events (bus `game`):** knockouts by weapon, crits, damage, streaks, the first knockout. Each event is stamped
  with the authority's clock: the tick of the snapshot that carried it / tickHz.
  - **Base Assault:** the authority sends a ball's `pickup` first, then its `ball taken` score. That pairing gives who
    grabbed which ball, and whether it came off the stand (a steal) or the ground.
  - `captured` is credited to that ball's carrier. A carrier's `death` is a stop for its killer (it arrives before
    the drop in the same tick).
  - Carry time runs from the grab to the capture, drop or return.
- **The roster** is authoritative: score, kills, deaths.
  - **Returns and pads have no event that names the player.** The Room credits the roster per player:
    - +100 a knockout;
    - a pickup's score, with a `pickup` event naming the taker;
    - Base Assault `ROSTER`: capture 10, steal 2 per grab, return 3;
    - Core Rush `captureBonus × 2` per pad taken.
  - What is left after the knockouts and pickups is objective credit:
    - pads = that / 10;
    - returns = (that − 10 × captures − 2 × grabs) / 3.
  - Both are claimed only when the stream covers the whole match (`complete`: the tally saw the warmup or a restart).
    Returns are claimed only when the division is exact; otherwise they count 0 and are never guessed.
  - A test pins those credit constants against the sim / Room source text.
- **The MatchState of every snapshot (bus `match`, which NetClient emits before that snapshot's events):** the phase,
  and the team score for UNDERDOG. A knockout is judged against the score before its own snapshot, so the tally reads
  the same whichever of the two comes first (tested both ways).
- **A stream joined mid-match** gives no FIRST BITE, no returns and no pads. Everything else still counts from the
  join.
- **Why per snapshot and the tick clock, not frames and `performance.now()`** (found in the real-game shots at ~1 fps):
  - fed per rendered frame, the first frame came after the warmup, so every stream read as joined mid-match (no
    FIRST BITE, no PAD PATROL on any card);
  - stamped on arrival, two Base Assault carries of about 64 and 60 s read as one 58 s MARATHON tie, because the page
    handles worker messages in bursts.

### Where the card sits (it joins the P2/U2 reward flow, it doesn't fight it)
The match-end flow is now:
1. **t = 0:** the WIN banner (top 32 %) and the XP reward card (bottom).
2. **t ≈ 0.5 s:** the awards card pops in **between them**. It is bottom-anchored 214 u up, above the tallest reward
   card; the cards pop in one by one, 0.18 s apart. It stays up 5.5 s and fades out in 0.5 s.
3. **Then the automatic scoreboard opens**, until the restart (hold 10 s).

The card sits **beneath** the scoreboard (z 7 vs 8), so **Tab still opens the scoreboard on top at any time**. It never
takes pointer, focus or keys. It hides when the next match starts. Reduced motion turns off the pop-ins. The card is
not used in adventure: the chapter card has the paws.

## 2. The kill feed
`killGlyph()` in `kill-feed.ts` (pure) decides the glyph:

1. `death.wpn` is a throwable (≥ `ORDNANCE_RULES.wireBase`) → its glyph (`squeaker_grenade` / `hairball_bomb`). This
   holds even for a self-knockout: a pet caught in its own blast shows the throwable, not the fall arrow.
2. Self → the fall arrow.
3. A gun `wpn` → the vehicle if the killer is seated (the plane's gun reports a hand gun), else that gun. A mortar kill
   now shows the mortar rather than the generic blast.
4. No `wpn`, or an index nobody knows (rams, abilities, boss swipes, a pre-W10 authority) → **exactly the pre-W10
   expression**. A test runs it as the oracle over every combination.

The throwable glyphs are X4's own HUD-slot drawings (`ordnance-hud.ts`), recoloured with the faction tape: corgi blue
`#2f6fd6`, cat crimson `#c9344a`.

## 3. Base Assault tips (the U1 FTUE system)
They run during the player's **first Base Assault match**: the first match of the mode with ≥ 10 s of play, whatever
came before. Each tip shows once, in the same slot and by the same rules as U1's (seen after 2 s on screen, a 3 s gap
between tips).

| Tip | When | Text |
|---|---|---|
| `ba_goal` | 4 s into the mode (after basics on a first-ever match); skipped if you already carried a ball | Steal their squeaky ball from its stand, run it home, touch your flag's ring to capture · first to 3 |
| `ba_return` | our ball is down; ends when it isn't | Our ball is down: touch it to send it home now, or it goes back by itself in 20 s |
| `ba_home` | they carry ours; ends when it's back | They have our ball! We can only capture while our own ball is home: stop their carrier |
| `ba_carry` | a teammate carries theirs; ends with the carry | Escort your carrier home: with the ball it runs slower and can't glide or ride |

- **While you carry,** G4a's carrier cue ("RUN IT HOME! / HOLD THE BALL") owns the slot and tips wait. The carry is
  still noted, so `ba_goal` is skipped.
- **Storage:** state persists under its own key, `cvc.tips.ba`. U1's `cvc.tips` record keeps its exact shape, which
  U1's tests pin.
- **Settings › Show again** resets both.
- The facts come from the snapshot's ball props (`baseAssaultTipFacts`).

## 7. Notes for Q5 and the lead
- **Test cost:** `ui-awards-room.test.ts` runs two short real matches: ~100 s and ~50 s of sim, with 120 s timeouts.
  That was ~20–50 s of wall time at load 16–24 here. It is the proof that the returns arithmetic holds on
  authoritative data.
- **A team switch mid-match:** the player gets a new body, and the old body's event stats are not merged into the new
  one. Their card may undercount, and returns may be withheld (the division isn't exact). The roster awards are
  unaffected.
- **A tie on GUARD DOG** shows the first winner's split (stops · returns) under a shared total.
- **Paste the hold together with the card.** Without the `holdScoreboard` hunk, the automatic scoreboard opens over
  the card at +2.5 s (seen in the lab before the lab passed the flag).
- **Pre-existing, not U3:** on a winning Base Assault capture, G4a's CAPTURED! burst (top 40 %) overlaps the HUD's WIN
  banner (top 32 %) (`awards-base-assault.png`).
- **Balance watch:** in small bot matches few players qualify, so the card fills with a second award for the same pet
  (e.g. BEST IN SHOW + HEAVY PAWS + ON A ROLL for one cat in a 5-knockout test match). Full 8-minute matches spread
  wider. The minimums live in `AWARD_RULES.min`.

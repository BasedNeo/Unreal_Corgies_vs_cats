# C2 handoff: Spotter Drone, Dig Charge, Squeak Barrier + bots that use abilities and play objectives

**Status: done and ready to integrate.** C2a (three authoritative ability entities + views + lab) and C2b (bots use
every class ability, hunt what drones spot, shoot enemy drones, run the Squeaker mission, take Upgrade Cores, and play
core-rush pads) are finished and tested. Main.ts needs one factory + two lines (§4.1). No shared contract was changed;
the only shared-content edit is the granted `live: true` flip for my three abilities (§5).

## 1. What players get

### Spotter Drone (Overwatch; cat "Bird Watcher")
- **Q** launches a toy quadcopter from above your head to a station **5 m ahead and 4.2 m up** (pulled short of walls
  and ceilings, kept ≥ 2.2 m above the ground). It hovers, bobs and spins for **6 s** (`duration`).
- Every 0.1 s it marks each enemy within **30 m** (`range`) that it can **see** (world line of sight from the drone,
  chest or head): `EFlag.Spotted` on that enemy, kept for 0.4 s after the last sighting.
  - Cloaked enemies are only spotted within 6 m of the drone; tall-grass concealment counts half (it looks down).
  - Your team sees a **red diamond reticle** over every spotted enemy, **through walls** (constant screen size past
    14 m). The other team sees nothing (the marker is only for the spotting team).
- It can be **shot down**: 60 hp, a 0.42 m sphere that stops bullets and projectiles (hit markers, `hit` events).
  Characters fly through it. It also goes down when its owner dies, leaves or changes team.
- Corgi drone: blue with gold trim and floppy ears. Cat drone: crimson "bird" with a beak and tail feathers.

### Dig Charge (Breacher; cat "Litter Mine")
- **Q** buries a charge on the ground under your crosshair (within 4.5 m), else at your feet. No ground → refused
  (no cooldown spent). At most 2 live charges each: a third makes the oldest fizzle.
- It **arms after 1 s**, then goes off when an enemy with a clear line to it comes within **3 m**, or when its
  **30 s fuse** (`duration`) runs out.
- The blast is the combat lane's own `explode()`: **70 damage** (`damage`) full inside 1 m, down to 30 % at **5 m**
  (`range`), knockback 9 m/s (`knockback`; knock-up 0.55 × = 5 m/s), owner takes 35 %. **Never through walls**:
  `explode()` traces from the blast center to every target (the same code as the tennis mortar). Barriers block it too.
- Enemy shots passing within 0.35 m of it, or enemy blasts nearby, **defuse** it (a pop, no blast).
- Allies see the full bomb, a **3 m trigger ring** and a red armed blink. **Enemies see it faintly**: only the top of
  the bomb pokes out of its mound, with a dim, rare blink.

### Squeak Barrier (Warden; cat "Scratch Wall")
- **Q** raises a wall **2 m in front of you, facing your aim**: 4 m wide (`range`), 2.4 m tall (plus 0.5 m buried to
  cover slopes), 0.4 m deep, for **8 s** (`duration`), **300 hp**.
- It is a fixed Rapier cuboid the **character controller of both teams** collides with (and karts), and it stops
  **hitscan, projectiles, bark blasts, explosions and bot sight lines**. Double-jumpers can hop it.
- Placement never overlaps a character or a solid prop: 11 nearby spots are tried (nearer/farther/sideways); if none is
  clear, flat enough (±0.5 m across its width) and on your side of any fence, it is **refused** (no cooldown, no event).
- Corgi wall: four inflatable squeaky pillows (blue/gold) with squeak buttons. Cat wall: four sisal scratching posts
  with crimson bands and a carpeted top plank. Pops up with an overshoot, squashes and flashes on hits, wobbles more
  as it weakens, sinks away on expiry, bursts flat when destroyed.

### Rules for all three
- Authoritative (`src/sim/combat/ability-*.ts`), deterministic (`sim.rng` only; a repeat run is bit-identical — test).
- Cleared silently whenever combat is not live (warmup, match over, restart). Drones also end with their owner.
- Cooldowns from `ABILITIES` (counted from activation; Squeaky Clean still halves them — the interact lane's drain).
- Client prediction does not run abilities: the entities arrive in snapshots.

### Bots (C2b)
| Class | When a bot uses its ability |
|---|---|
| Assault — bark blast | An enemy it can perceive within reach in front (never a cloaked/grass-hidden one it hasn't seen). In PvP modes, room bots with it ready **rush a reloading target** (always) or, on a roll, any target within 22 m (hip-firing, no ADS, 4 s). |
| Infiltrator — cloak | Investigating a noise 10–35 m away (sneak up, 70 %), hurt below half health (break off to cover), and the old retreat case. |
| Overwatch — drone | Searching: patrolling 2.5 s / investigating 0.75 s with nobody in sight, or a target lost for 0.75 s; one drone at a time. |
| Breacher — charge | A pursuer while retreating, an enemy closing in (or trading shots < 12 m), on the way to a noise (chase path), inside a hold zone, in a narrow passage (nav chokepoint test). |
| Warden — barrier | Shot at in the open from 6–40 m (the shooter can see it): turns to the shooter, walls up, holds cover behind it for 2.5 s. |
| Skyraider — ear glide | Any jump becomes a double jump at the apex + glide (reads `char.glideCooldown`/`glideTime`); long patrol trips start a hop. |

- PvE wave cats roll `abilityUse` (grunt 0.5, sniper 0.6, brute 0.7, kitten 0.35) and **hold their tricks until wave 3**
  so the opening stays gentle. Room bots use 1.0. Player stand-ins (external brains) never do the bark rush.
- **Spotted enemies**: a room bot with nobody in sight goes to investigate the nearest enemy its team's drones spot
  (it still needs its own line of sight to shoot). PvE waves don't (they already hunt).
- **Drones get shot down**: a bot with no character target aims at an enemy drone in reach and sight (hitscan,
  non-charge kits).
- **Bots stick with their human**: a room bot goes to help a human teammate hurt in the last 3 s within 90 m (toward
  the shooter), and doesn't leave for cores, interact steps or hold zones while that human is in trouble. In the
  skirmish the squad also starts the objective chain only from wave 2. (Without these, the co-op squad left the
  player alone early and the first death dropped — see §6.)
- **Objectives** (walked to in patrol, fought from while engaged):
  - Squeaker chain (its team only): the team's lowest-id bot is the **runner** for interact steps (walks to the nearest
    walkable cell, then straight into reach, presses E — never within 3.2 m of a kart or terminal); an interact point
    in enemy ground waits for a lull (no enemy within 30 m of it) unless the runner is within 25 m; the runner keeps
    heading for the point while trading shots with enemies > 18 m away.
  - Hold steps: every team bot takes a walkable spot in the zone and **fights, investigates and takes cover from
    inside it**; breachers mine it.
  - Upgrade Cores: room bots with ≥ 50 % hp walk to an available core within 30 m on walkable ground (the perches need
    platforming, so they're skipped), via the spot's lawn route, then straight in; unreachable cores are skipped 20 s.
  - **core-rush** (the lead's request): every other bot of a team attacks the best pad it doesn't own (near,
    contested or being taken scores higher); the rest defend owned pads under attack, else sit on the nearest owned
    pad. Pads are hold zones: bots fight from inside them.

## 3. Snapshot conventions (existing `EntityState` fields only)
EntityKind.Prop (`ABILITY_ENTITY_KIND`), `cls` = ABILITY_IDS index (2 drone · 3 charge · 4 barrier), `team`/`species`
= owner's, `seed` = owner id, `yaw` = facing, `hp/maxHp` = health (charge 0/0), `ammo` = whole seconds left,
`flags & Busy` = drone launching / charge ARMED. Drone: `x,y,z` = center, `vx,vy,vz` = velocity, `weapon` = enemies
spotted now. Charge: `x,y,z` = planted point. Barrier: `x,y,z` = ground under its center (spans y − 0.5 … y + 2.4).
Events: `ability { id: owner, ability: <id> }` on deploy (starts the HUD ring; the onomatopoeia already has ZAP! /
BONK! / SQUEAK!), `ability { id: entity, ability: '<id>:down' | '<id>:end' }` on destroy/expiry, `hit { dst: entity }`
when shot, `explode { by: owner }` when a charge blows. No new event types.

## 4. Wiring for the lead
### 4.1 `src/client/main.ts`
```ts
import { createAbilityViews } from './abilities';
// next to the interact views:
const abilityViews = createAbilityViews(ctx.scene, { camera: ctx.camera }); // C2: drones, charges, barriers, spotted markers
// in bus.on('game', (ev) => { … }):
abilityViews.onGameEvent(ev);
// frame loop, after interact.sync(states, pdt):
abilityViews.sync(states, (states.get(localId)?.team ?? -1) as TeamId | -1, pdt);
```
Optional "you are spotted" cue, like the HIDDEN one (HUD lane may prefer its own):
```ts
spottedCue.style.display = local && (local.flags & EFlag.Spotted) ? 'block' : 'none';
```

### 4.2 Client prediction (`src/client/net/prediction.ts`, lead-owned) — recommended
Predicted movement doesn't know barriers, so the local player walks into a wall and is pulled back by reconciliation.
`mirrorBarriers` adds/removes the authority's exact barrier colliders in the predictor's world (tested: a predicted
runner stops at the wall):
```ts
import type { Collider } from '@dimforge/rapier3d-compat';
import { mirrorBarriers } from '../../sim/combat/ability-barrier';
// LocalPredictor:
private barriers = new Map<number, Collider>();
/** Call with each snapshot's entity states (before replaying inputs). */
syncWorld(states: Iterable<EntityState>): void { mirrorBarriers(this.sim, states, this.barriers); }
```

### 4.3 Optional sim wiring
`abilityEntitySystem` registers itself on the first ability-system update (`Sim.addSystem`). To list it up front, add
it to `combatSystems()` in `src/sim/combat/index.ts` (`import { abilityEntitySystem } from './ability-entities'`);
its `init` marks it registered, so it never runs twice.

## 7. Known issues
- `fire` events carry pellet 0 only: spread weapons hitting a drone/barrier use a pellet factor (0.85 × pellets on a
  wall, 0.35 × on a drone); a tapped (uncharged) laser counts full damage against them (no charge fraction in the event).
- Friendly shots are stopped by your own barrier/drone (they're physical colliders); they take no damage from them.
- The nav grid is static: bots path into barriers and get around them with the stuck detour (≤ 1.5 s).
- Until §4.2 is wired, the local player gets reconciliation corrections at barriers.
- The drone stays at its station (it doesn't follow the owner).
- Bots only shoot drones with hitscan non-charge kits (snipers, mortars and frisbees ignore them).
- The Squeaker's last step (the Cat Tree) is deep in cat ground: with an AFK human the bots finish the whole chain in
  1 of 2 seeds within 480 s (both seeds finish grab + hold).
- Pre-existing (B1): the boss regrade drains and re-emits events, so soak/qa tools double-count events while a boss is
  alive; §5.1 would let it read in place too.
- An AFK human always under fire at the spawn anchors the squad (bots help a human in trouble first): with 2 bots + an
  AFK human the squad loses a little earlier than on HEAD (seed 1: 234 s vs 299 s) and does less of the mission. A
  "human is active" test (recent input) would fix it; I left it simple.
- Kiosk play for bots (kit swaps) is not implemented (the card listed cores + mission; kits would need a counter-pick
  policy).

- **Breaching fuse.** A Dig Charge planted within 2.6 m of a standing breach wall (either side) blows 1.5 s after it
  arms, instead of waiting for an enemy or its 30 s fuse. The charge stays C2's entity; only its deadline (`abx.until`)
  is shortened, so C2's own system detonates it through `explode()`.
- **Blast rules.**
  - Blasts use `explode()`'s own falloff.
  - The line of sight is traced to the closest point of the prop, ignoring the prop's own colliders. Cover blocks it:
    a blast outside the garage wall never hurts a stack inside.
  - The blast that breaks a wall still shielded everyone behind it: destructibles take the blast after the characters.
- **Kart wrecks, boss blasts and mortars** all go through the same hook.
- **Collisions.** Standing props are World-layer colliders. Characters, karts, shots, projectiles, blasts and bot sight
  lines all collide with them.
- **No floating debris colliders.** Debris is client-only. Rubble has no colliders and is ≤ 0.3 m tall.
- **Combat must be live.** Damage counts only while combat is live, so nothing breaks during warmup or after the match
  ends.
- **Breaks are heard.** A break is a noise bots hear: 60 m for the wall, 30–35 m for stacks.

## 4. How it works (for reviewers)

### Sim (`destructSystem`, order 505)
- **Init.** For each destructible, the system spawns an entity (§8) and creates its World-layer box colliders
  (`WORLD_GROUPS`, tagged). It also registers a nav blocker.
- **Each tick:**
  1. A match restart stands everything back up. The system detects a restart the way S1's interact layer does: a new
     `sim.state.match` object, or `ended` → anything else. A1's chapter start makes a new MatchState, so each chapter
     starts with everything standing.
  2. New Dig Charges near a standing breach wall get the breaching fuse.
  3. This tick's hitscan `fire` events are read in place (`peekEvents`, a WeakSet of seen events, the C2 pattern). A
     ray that stopped (`hit -1`) within 0.12 m of a stack's box damages it:
     `damage × falloff × pellets (×0.85 for spread weapons) × shotMult`.
- **Blasts.** `addBlastListener(onBlast)` runs at the end of every `explode()`.

### Break and restore
- **Break:**
  - hp 0 + `EFlag.Busy`; the colliders are removed;
  - `setNavBlocker(key, null)` merges the freed cells into their regions;
  - a `BreakRecord` is stored;
  - unless silent: the `ability { id, ability: 'destruct:<kind>', x, y, z }` event (at the prop's center) and a noise.
- **Restore:**
  - full hp, flags cleared, colliders re-created, the nav blocker is back;
  - any character whose capsule overlaps a box steps out through its nearest side face.

### Nav (`src/sim/ai/nav.ts`)
- **The shared grid ignores destructibles.** It stays cached per world and shared by every Sim: its collider count and
  its Rapier probes skip tagged colliders, so it is the world with everything broken.
- **Per-sim copy.** A sim with blockers gets its own copy of `walk` / `region` from `navGridFor()`.
  - Ground, costs and the A* scratch stay shared; the search stamp is now a global counter.
  - The copy is **updated in place**, because bots hold on to the grid object they got at tick 1.
- **Blocked cells.** `blockedCells(grid, boxes)` returns the cells whose build probe would touch the boxes. The probe is
  the build's capsule 0.45 m over the ground; the test is analytic for yaw-only boxes.
- **Opening vs closing.**
  - Opening (a break) floods the freed cells and folds the regions they touch into the largest one. A test proves it
    equals a full relabel.
  - Closing (a restore) relabels everything once; `batchNavBlockers` makes a reset do it once.

### Client
- **Merged meshes.** Standing and rubble prims of all 8 destructibles are merged into one mesh per render group
  (solid / soft / noink) plus one crease LineSegments2.
- **Breaking is a buffer edit.** Each destructible owns index ranges in those buffers, plus segment ranges in the ink
  buffer:
  - hiding zeroes its triangles (degenerate) and parks its ink segments at y = -1e4;
  - showing copies the original ranges back;
  - the update is a ranged upload: no rebuild, no allocation.
- **The break event** switches the prop to rubble at once and bursts the debris.
- **Snapshots** handle the rest:
  - a late joiner sees rubble with no debris;
  - a missed event still breaks the prop, with debris if the break counter moved while we watched;
  - a reset stands it back up, ignored for 1.5 s after a break event, because interpolated states lag the event.
- **Debris.**
  - Preallocated pools: 180 chunks, 48 cans, recycled when full.
  - Chunks bounce on a height map baked once around each prop, from terrain + props, so they land on the garage slab or
    the car.
  - The break path is warmed at load, so the first break costs the same as later ones.

## 5. The A2 hook: counting `destroy` triggers by tag
A1's default probe already reads X1's entities correctly: `e.name` is the def id, so it finds the tag in
`WorldData.destructibles`, and broken means `hp <= 0`. For an exact reader:
```ts
import { setDestructibleProbe } from '../adventure/state';
setDestructibleProbe((sim, e) => (e.dsx ? { tag: e.dsx.def.tag, broken: e.dsx.broken } : null));
```

**X1 API** (`src/sim/destruct/index.ts`), all deterministic:

| Call | Use |
|---|---|
| `destructibleCount(sim, tag, 'broken' \| 'standing' \| 'all')` | Chapter 3 `destroy { tag: 'garage_breach_wall', count: 1 }` and `destroy { tag: 'tuna_stack', count: 4 }`: done when `destructibleCount(sim, tag) >= count` |
| `destructiblesByTag(sim, tag)` | Beacon targets: `e.dsx.def.cx / cy / cz` is the center, `e.pos` the ground anchor |
| `drainBreaks(sim)` | `BreakRecord { id, defId, tag, kind, by, byTeam, tick, scripted }`: barks ("the wall's down!"), who blew it |
| `destructSnapshot(sim)` / `restoreDestructSnapshot(sim, ids)` | **Checkpoints.** Store the broken def ids with the checkpoint; on `restartAtCheckpoint`, restore exactly that (silent: no events or debris). Otherwise stacks broken after the checkpoint stay broken while the counters roll back, and the step can never finish. |
| `resetDestructibles(sim, tags?)` · `breakDestructibles(sim, tagOrId, { silent, by })` · `damageDestructible(sim, e, dmg, by)` | Scripting |

## 7. Known issues
- **Reset relabel.** A reset relabels the sim's nav regions once: ~4–9 ms under this machine's load, on a match or
  checkpoint restart only. Breaks are incremental (≤ 0.3 ms).
- **Kart ramming.** A kart ramming a stack doesn't break it; the kart just collides. Kart **wrecks** (explosions) do
  break stacks.
- **Hitscan events carry pellet 0 only.** Spread weapons use C2's pellet factor. An uncharged laser tap counts full
  damage.
- **Projectiles.** Frisbees don't damage stacks. Only hitscan and explosions do.
- **Rubble is visual.** Characters walk through the low rubble; it is ≤ 0.3 m tall.
- **Bots don't breach on their own yet.** That is A2 (tactics). Bots do path through the hole once it's open.
- **Triangles.** The destructibles' merged buffers hold ~35 k triangles, standing + rubble; hidden triangles are
  degenerate. The tuna cans are already 12-segment. Draw calls: 3 meshes + 1 ink set, +2 instanced meshes while
  debris flies.
- **Late joiners.** The breach can't be told from the other doors on the 1 m nav grid until it breaks, and the doorway
  is 5 cells wide. A late joiner who arrives mid-debris sees the rubble without the burst (by design).
